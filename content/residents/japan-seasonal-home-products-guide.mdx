## Why seasonal products become part of life in Japan

| Season pressure | What your home needs |
| --- | --- |
| **Humid summers** | Dry air paths, mold control |
| **Rainy weeks** | Indoor laundry, moisture traps |
| **Pollen spring** | Filters, masks, laundry habits |
| **Cold dry winter** | Heat without gas bills exploding |
| **Typhoon days** | Tape, lights, towel stock |

Japan apartments are often **compact and lightly insulated**. The climate does the rest.

**Foreign residents underestimate this:** Thinking one AC unit and one heater solve everything. Comfort is usually **a stack of small tools**, not one expensive machine.

**Scenario:** June in Nagoya. Humidity 80%. Clothes smell almost dry. You buy a **compressor dehumidifier** and a **laundry deodorizer**. October feels easy by comparison.

## Air circulators and apartment airflow

| Device | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Circulator fan (サーキュレーター)** | Move AC cold air evenly |
| **Standard fan** | Cheaper, less focused |
| **Exhaust fan (existing)** | Kitchen, bath |
| **Door gap wedge** | Optional airflow tuning |

**Home comfort scenario:** Summer night in 1K. AC on 27°C. Air sits near ceiling. **Circulator** points along ceiling line. Sleep without blasting 22°C.

**Foreign residents underestimate this:** A circulator can **lower perceived heat** more than lowering AC temperature 2 degrees.

## FAQ

### What should I buy first after moving in?

Dehumidifier or circulator depending on season, drying rack, bathroom mold spray, spare umbrella.

### Are kotatsu worth it in a small apartment?

Many residents say yes for winter evenings. Check voltage, size, and fire safety labels.

### Do I need an air purifier?

Helpful for pollen and city dust. Not mandatory if masks and laundry habits work.

### Which dehumidifier type for Tokyo summer?

Compressor type for warm humid rooms. Desiccant for cold damp closets.

### Can I survive without AC?

Possible in mild regions. Tokyo summers are harsh for most foreigners without AC or strong fan plan.

### Where do residents buy seasonal items cheap?

Daiso, drugstores, home centers, seasonal sales at Nitori and Amazon.

### How do I store off-season products?

Vacuum bags, under-bed boxes, label with month. Clean appliances before storage.

### Are heated blankets safe?

Buy labeled products, no damaged cords, follow timer instructions.

### Do apartments include bathroom dryer?

Many newer units yes. Older units need rack strategy.
