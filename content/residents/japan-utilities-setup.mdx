## Quick answer: How do I set up utilities after moving?

**Start electricity and gas before move-in day if possible.** Water often transfers with address registration. Internet is a separate order.

| Utility | First action |
| --- | --- |
| Electricity | Call or web start service on move-in date |
| Gas | Book activation visit (city gas) |
| Water | Confirm with landlord or city water bureau |
| Internet | Apply 2-4 weeks early |

**Quick answer:** Keep utility confirmation emails and first bills in one folder. Official mail: [official mail guide](/residents/japan-official-mail-guide).

## Setup timeline

| When | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 2 weeks before | Apply internet, note gas type |
| 1 week before | Start electricity transfer |
| Move-in day | Be home for gas tech if required |
| Week 1 | Register auto-pay or konbini pay habit |

## Electricity

| Topic | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Providers | Regional retailers (TEPCO, KEPCO arms, etc.) |
| Start service | Phone or web with address and move date |
| All-electric mansion | No gas, IH stove |
| Bills | Monthly postcard or email |

## Gas

| Type | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| **City gas (都市ガス)** | Technician unlocks meter, safety check |
| **LP gas (プロパン)** | Tank building, landlord coordinates |
| Appointment | Someone 18+ must be home |

| Gas anxiety | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| "Did I break something?" | Valve off until activation is normal |
| Missed appointment | Reschedule, eat out one more day |

## Water

| Pattern | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Included in rent | Confirm with lease |
| Separate bill | City water bureau registration |
| Hot water | Gas or electric depending on unit |

## Utility provider comparison

| Utility | Who you contact | Typical bill rhythm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electricity | Retailer you choose or default | Monthly |
| Gas | City gas company or LP vendor | Monthly |
| Water | Municipal water bureau | Every 2 months in some cities |
| Internet | ISP separate | Monthly |

## FAQ

### Are utilities in my name or landlord's?

Lease defines. "Utilities included" may mean water only.

### Can I set up utilities without Japanese?

Web forms vary. Phone with translation app works.

### Gas activation how long?

Appointment often 1-2 weeks out in busy seasons.

### Cancel when moving out?

Stop service on move-out date to avoid overlap bills.
