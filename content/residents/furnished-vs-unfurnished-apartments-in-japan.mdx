## Quick answer: Furnished or unfurnished?

| Type | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Unfurnished standard lease** | Stay 1+ years, want lowest rent |
| **Furnished monthly mansion** | First months, short assignments |
| **Company housing** | Employer provides basics |

**Quick answer:** Budget ¥100,000-¥300,000+ setup for a true unfurnished 1K if you need everything. Furnished monthly costs more rent, less shopping stress.

## What "unfurnished" actually means in Japan

| Often missing | Sometimes present |
| --- | --- |
| Bed, sofa, desk | AC unit (not always) |
| Curtains | Built-in kitchen (compact) |
| Lights (fixtures only) | Bathroom unit |
| Washing machine | Fridge (rare in cheap units) |
| Microwave, rice cooker | |

"Unfurnished" can mean **four walls and utilities hookups**, not Western "white goods included."

<InfoBox title="Most foreign residents misunderstand this">
  - **"Unfurnished includes fridge."** Often no.
  - **"I can buy everything at one store."** Plan IKEA, Nitori, Amazon, recycle shop routes.
  - **"Furnished lease is always cheaper total."** Higher rent may exceed buying used and keeping.
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Are furnished apartments available for 1-year lease?

Monthly mansions yes. Standard furnished yearly leases exist but less common.

### Can I negotiate appliances?

Sometimes for older buildings. Ask agency.

### Rent unfurnished then buy used?

Common money-saving path for long stays.

### What about lighting?

Ceiling mount may be bulb only. Floor lamps fastest fix.
