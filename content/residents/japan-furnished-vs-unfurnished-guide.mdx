## Why apartment setups in Japan surprise foreigners

| Expectation abroad | Japan reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Unfurnished = fridge stays** | Appliances may be missing |
| **Furnished easy to find** | Smaller share of market |
| **Month-to-month common** | Annual lease culture |
| **Leave furniture behind** | Disposal fees apply |
| **Big rooms** | Furnish carefully |
| **Utilities included** | Usually separate |

Housing is a **bundle of rent plus setup cost**, not rent alone.

## What “unfurnished” actually means in Japan

Listings use labels like **家具なし** (no furniture) or **家電なし** (no appliances). Read both.

| Label | Usually means |
| --- | --- |
| **家具なし** | No bed, table, sofa |
| **家電付き** | Fridge, washer, sometimes AC |
| **エアコン付き** | AC units installed |
| **照明付き** | Ceiling lights only |
| **完全空室** | Truly empty |

| Often stays in unit | Often you buy |
| --- | --- |
| Kitchen counter and sink | Bed |
| Unit bath | Sofa |
| Closet rods | Dining table |
| Flooring | Washer |
| Intercom | Microwave |

Guarantor and lease context: [guarantor system](/residents/japan-guarantor-system). Search help: [renting an apartment](/residents/renting-apartment-japan).

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents misunderstand this">
  **"Unfurnished" does not mean "move-in ready" in Japan.** Budget **furniture plus appliances** unless the listing explicitly lists each item.
</InfoBox>

## Furnished apartment options explained

**Furnished (家具付き / ファニチャー付き)** listings include basic furniture. Level varies.

| Included sometimes | Verify in contract |
| --- | --- |
| Bed and mattress | Size |
| Table and chairs | Count |
| Sofa or floor chairs | Condition |
| Curtains | Fit windows |
| Small appliances | Which models |

| Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- |
| Fast move-in | Higher rent |
| Good for 1-2 year stay | Wear charged on exit |
| Less disposal on leave | Style not yours |
| English agents sometimes | Limited inventory |

| Who it fits | Why |
| --- | --- |
| First 6-12 months in Japan | Speed |
| Corporate relocation package | Employer pays |
| Students in approved units | Campus partnerships |

## Monthly mansions and short-term rentals

**Monthly mansion (マンスリーマンション)** and **weekly mansions** are furnished, often with utilities and Wi-Fi bundled.

| Feature | Typical |
| --- | --- |
| Lease | 1 month minimum, sometimes 7 days |
| Furniture | Full basic set |
| Utilities | Often included cap |
| Internet | Sometimes included |
| Deposit | Lower or none |
| Guarantor | Sometimes not required |

| Tradeoff | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Rent per month | 1.5-2.5x similar unfurnished area |
| Size | Compact |
| Availability | Tokyo and business districts strong |

**Scenario:** Consultant on 8-month assignment in Minato. Monthly mansion ¥180,000 all-in vs unfurnished ¥120,000 plus ¥150,000 setup. Short stay favors mansion.

## Furniture disposal and recycling realities

Leaving Japan or upgrading means **paying to discard**, not leaving on curb.

| Item | Disposal |
| --- | --- |
| Sofa | Sodai gomi sticker |
| Mattress | Often sodai gomi |
| Fridge | Retailer take-back fee |
| Small goods | Mercari sell first |

| --- | --- |

## FAQ

### Are furnished apartments common in Japan?

Less common than abroad. Available in cities, often at premium rent.

### What does unfurnished include in Tokyo?

Varies. Often kitchen and bath fixtures only. Confirm AC, washer, fridge in writing.

### Are monthly mansions worth it?

Good for under 12 months or corporate short stay. Higher monthly cost.

### Can I leave furniture when I move out?

Usually no. Dispose properly or sell. Landlord may charge cleaning.

### Do I need a guarantor for monthly mansions?

Sometimes no. Read contract.

### Is it cheaper to buy used furniture?

Yes for stays over 12 months. Recycle shops and Mercari help.

### Who owns the air conditioner?

Often tenant buys and installs. Removal cost on exit.

### Can families find furnished 2LDK?

Harder. Plan unfurnished plus delivery for large items.

### Is internet included in furnished rent?

Sometimes in monthly mansions. Rare in standard furnished leases.

### What is the cheapest first-month setup?

Used bed, fridge, washer, microwave, rice cooker from recycle shops.

### Should students choose furnished?

If dorm unavailable, furnished or share house reduces stress.

### How do I compare listings fairly?

Make a spreadsheet: rent, fees, included appliances, commute, floor size.
