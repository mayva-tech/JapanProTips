## FAQ

### What are micro seasons in Japan?

The **small shifts** between major seasons: early plum bloom, pre-rain humidity, first oden week, not only cherry and snow.

### Do convenience stores really change that early?

Often yes. **Marketing and supply** run ahead of weather by days or weeks.

### Is seasonal living only for Japanese culture fans?

No. It is **default retail and commute life** everyone experiences.

### How do I track seasons practically?

Follow **supermarket produce**, konbini displays, and your **laundry drying habit**.

### Does Tokyo represent all Japan?

No. **Hokkaido and Okinawa** differ strongly. This guide leans urban mainland rhythm.

### Can seasonal shifts affect mood?

Yes. Light, humidity, and social calendar all play a role. Seek support if low mood persists.

### What should I buy each season first?

Masks and laundry sheets in spring, dehumidifier in rain, AC filter in summer, lip balm and heat packs in winter.
