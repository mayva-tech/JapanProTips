## Why clothing shopping in Japan surprises foreigners

| Expectation | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **US size label matches** | Japanese L can feel like US S-M |
| **Mall variety** | Chains dominate, less mid-tier |
| **Cheap fast fashion only** | Quality basics at Uniqlo are normal |
| **Casual office everywhere** | Many offices still formal |
| **Dry cleaning optional** | Humidity and small washers matter |
| **Big and tall easy** | Limited racks, online helps |

You are shopping for **climate and norms**, not only style.

## Japanese sizing differences explained

Japan uses **letter sizes (S/M/L)** and **number sizes (7, 9, 11)** for women, **centimeter measurements** for men pants, and **mondo (mm)** for shoes.

| Women's letter | Rough Japan fit | Foreign note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | 5-7号 | Petite |
| M | 9-11号 | Often small vs US M |
| L | 13-15号 | Not US L |
| XL+ | Limited in stores | Online or import |

| Men's pants | Waist cm on tag |
| --- | --- |
| S | 71-76 |
| M | 77-82 |
| L | 83-88 |
| XL | 89-96 (harder in store) |

| Sizing example | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| US woman size 8 dress | Often Japan L or XL top |
| US man 32 waist | Japan M-L pants |
| UK shoe 9 men | Japan 27-27.5 cm |

| Tip | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Check **実寸** on tag | Garment cm, not body |
| Size up one | Safe first buy |
| Uniqlo size chart app | Compare before cart |

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents get surprised by this">
  **A Japanese XL shirt is often smaller than a US medium in shoulders.** Letter sizes are not portable across countries.
</InfoBox>

## Business clothing expectations in Japan

Norms vary by industry. **Finance, government, traditional corporate** lean formal. **Tech, creative, English teaching** lean casual.

| Level | Men | Women |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Formal** | Dark suit, white shirt, conservative tie | Suit set, closed shoes, subtle jewelry |
| **Business casual** | Chinos, collared shirt, blazer optional | Blouse, knee skirt or slacks, flats ok |
| **Casual office** | Clean jeans sometimes, no holes | Smart casual, no beach wear |
| **Teaching / startup** | Polo or neat tee, clean shoes | Modest neckline common |

| Detail | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Shoes** | Leather or clean sneakers, not gym shoes |
| **Grooming** | Hair neat, light fragrance |
| **Bags** | Business tote, not backpack sometimes |
| **Tattoos** | Cover in client-facing roles |

## FAQ

### Is Uniqlo enough for office work?

Often yes with chinos and collared shirts. Formal industries need suits.

### What size should I buy in Japan if I wear US M?

Often L or XL tops. Pants: check waist cm. Try on.

### Where can I find plus-size clothes?

Online, Workman XXL, H&M, import shops in big cities.

### Are Japanese shoes smaller?

Lengths use cm mondo. Width can feel narrow.

### How do I dry clothes in a small apartment?

Indoor rack, bathroom fan, coin laundry dryer for heavy items.

### Is secondhand clothing common?

Yes. Chains are clean and popular.

### Do I need a suit in Japan?

Depends on job. Banking and formal corporate yes. Tech often no.

### What should I wear in summer humidity?

Light synthetic or linen, carry towel, spare shirt.

### Can I shop in English?

Flagship stores sometimes. GU and Shimamura less.

### How often do people wash clothes?

Every 1-2 wears in summer for tops. Jackets less often.

### Is Zozotown worth it?

Good selection if you read size advice and return policy.

### What about laundry for suits?

Dry clean or office district cleaning services.
