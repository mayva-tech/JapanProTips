## Why healthcare costs in Japan confuse foreigners

| Confusion | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Low copay vs high US bills** | 30% feels cheap, adds up yearly |
| **Separate pharmacy** | Two stops, two payments |
| **No GP assigned** | You choose clinics |
| **Cash and card mix** | Not all clinics same |
| **Dental separate** | Often not fully covered |
| **Insurance card required** | Without it, full price |

You pay for **access and predictability**, not surprise $5,000 ER stories common elsewhere.

## How National Health Insurance changes costs

**National Health Insurance (NHI, 国民健康保険)** and **employee insurance (shakai hoken)** both use similar **copay ratios** for most care.

| Copay ratio | Who pays what |
| --- | --- |
| **30% (standard adult)** | You 30%, insurance 70% |
| **20% (children, varies)** | Age rules by municipality |
| **10% (high medical costs)** | After annual cap triggers |

| Example visit (insured) | Total fee | Your 30% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinic consultation | ¥3,000 | ¥900 |
| Blood test add-on | ¥2,000 | ¥600 |
| **Same day total** | ¥5,000 | **¥1,500** |

Enrollment guide: [National Health Insurance](/residents/japan-health-insurance).

## Typical clinic visit pricing

**Clinics (クリニック)** handle most colds, stomach issues, minor injuries.

| Visit type | Illustrative total (before insurance) | Your 30% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cold, 5 min consult** | ¥2,500-¥4,000 | ¥750-¥1,200 |
| **Stomach pain plus test** | ¥5,000-¥8,000 | ¥1,500-¥2,400 |
| **Dermatology first visit** | ¥3,000-¥6,000 | ¥900-¥1,800 |
| **Mental health consult** | ¥5,000-¥10,000+ | Varies |

| Factor | Price effect |
| --- | --- |
| **First visit fee (初診)** | Higher than follow-up (再診) |
| **Evening or holiday** | Surcharge possible |
| **Tests ordered** | X-ray, blood add cost |

**Scenario:** Fever on Tuesday. Internal medicine clinic near station. Total ¥4,200, you pay ¥1,260 at desk with insurance card.

## Prescription medication costs

Prescriptions are filled at **pharmacies (薬局)**, not the clinic desk.

| Item | Illustrative insured cost (your share) |
| --- | --- |
| **Cold medicine pack** | ¥300-¥800 |
| **Antibiotics short course** | ¥500-¥1,500 |
| **Chronic blood pressure monthly** | ¥500-¥2,000 |
| **Skin cream** | ¥200-¥600 |

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Doctor issues prescription slip |
| 2 | Go to pharmacy same day |
| 3 | Hand slip plus insurance card |
| 4 | Pay copay, receive bag |

Generic drugs (**ジェネリック**) cost less. Ask ジェネリックはありますか if you want savings.

## Hospitals vs clinics explained

| | **Clinic** | **Hospital (病院)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scale** | Small, local | Larger, departments |
| **Referral** | Walk-in common | Often need introduction (紹介状) |
| **Cost** | Lower base | Higher facility fees |
| **Wait** | Shorter usually | Longer, especially specialists |
| **Use for** | Cold, mild issues | Serious, imaging, surgery |

Without referral, some hospitals charge **extra non-referred fee** on top of usual copay.

## Dental care realities

Dental is covered for **basic treatment** under insurance, but many residents still feel it is pricey.

| Treatment | Insurance note | Illustrative your share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Checkup, cleaning (covered basics)** | Twice yearly logic | ¥1,000-¥3,000 |
| **Cavity filling** | Covered within rules | ¥1,500-¥4,000 |
| **Whitening, cosmetic** | Not covered | Full private price |
| **Orthodontics** | Limited, often partial | High out of pocket |

Dental offices often explain **covered vs not covered** before work. Ask for estimate.

## Emergency room expectations

**ER (救急)** is for serious symptoms, not mild cold.

| Situation | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Chest pain, stroke signs | ER or call 119 |
| High fever infant | Pediatric ER |
| Minor sprain | Clinic or orthopedics |
| Late night fever | Night clinic if available |

| Cost reality | Note |
| --- | --- |
| ER visit insured | Still 30%, but total fee higher |
| Ambulance | Often low cost with insurance, municipality rules |

Emergency flow: [emergency guide](/residents/japan-emergency-guide).

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents get surprised by">
  **Ambulance rides are not US-priced.** Insurance and local rules keep them manageable. Still call 119 for real emergencies.
</InfoBox>

## Specialist referrals and waiting systems

| Path | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Clinic refers to hospital** | Introduction letter (紹介状) |
| **Direct to specialist** | Possible, may cost more |
| **Appointment wait** | Weeks for popular specialists |
| **Online booking** | Some clinics use apps |

Bring **insurance card, ID, medication list** every time.

## Health insurance card usage

Show **insurance card (保険証)** at:

| Location | When |
| --- | --- |
| Clinic reception | Check-in |
| Pharmacy | Pickup |
| Hospital admin | Admission |

Digital card rollout varies. Carry **physical card** until your municipality says otherwise.

Late enrollment without card may mean **full price** until back enrollment resolved.

## What insurance usually does NOT fully cover

| Item | Typical status |
| --- | --- |
| **Cosmetic dental** | Not covered |
| **Premium room hospital bed** | Extra daily charge |
| **Some vaccines** | Partial or self-pay |
| **Over-the-counter without script** | Full price at drugstore |
| **Health check extras** | Optional panels self-pay |
| **Eyeglasses** | Not medical insurance |

| High-cost relief | **High medical expense system (高額療養費)** caps monthly out-of-pocket by income |

Ask clinic about **限度額認定証** if chronic illness expected.

## Preventive care and annual checkups

| Check | Cost pattern |
| --- | --- |
| **NHI annual checkup (特定健診)** | Low or free by age |
| **Company health exam** | Employer paid |
| **Extra cancer screening** | Optional fee |

Worth doing. Catches issues early, cheaper long term.

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Going to **ER for cold** (long wait, higher fee)
- Forgetting insurance card (pay full, fix later with paperwork)
- Not registering NHI after move (illegal gap, full bills)
- Assuming **US insurance** covers routine Japan care (usually no)
- Skipping **pharmacy** after visit (prescription expires)
- Choosing **non-covered dental** without asking price
- Ignoring **referral letter** for hospital specialist

## What Japan does differently from other countries

| Topic | Japan | Many other systems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gatekeeper GP** | Weak, you choose | Strong in UK |
| **Price transparency** | Receipt shows lines | Varies |
| **Insurance tied to residence** | Municipal NHI | Varies |
| **Pharmacy split** | Almost always separate | Often combined |
| **Copay flat %** | 30% common | Deductibles US style |

The system rewards **showing up early at clinic** before problems escalate.

## FAQ

### How much is a doctor visit with insurance?

Often ¥800-¥2,000 out of pocket for simple visit. Tests add more.

### Is healthcare free in Japan?

No. You pay copay plus monthly insurance premium.

### Do I need insurance card every time?

Yes. Proves coverage at clinic and pharmacy.

### Are hospitals more expensive?

Generally yes than clinics. Serious care costs more total.

### Is dental covered?

Basic treatment yes. Cosmetic and orthodontics often not.

### Can I use credit card at clinic?

Many urban clinics yes. Carry cash backup.

### What if I am uninsured tourist vs resident?

Residents must enroll. Tourists need travel insurance.

### How do prescriptions work?

Doctor gives paper or electronic script. Pharmacy fills with copay.

### Is mental health covered?

Yes under insurance, clinic availability varies.

### What is high medical expense cap?

Monthly limit on copay by income. Apply if costs spike.
