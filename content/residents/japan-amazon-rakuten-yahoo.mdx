## Quick answer: Which online shopping platform is easiest?

**For most newcomers, Amazon Japan is the easiest first platform.** English UI options, familiar checkout, and predictable delivery for everyday items.

| Platform | First-week friendliness |
| --- | --- |
| **Amazon Japan** | High |
| **Rakuten** | Low until you learn the layout |
| **Yahoo Shopping** | Medium, often used with Yahoo Japan ID |

**Quick answer:** Use Amazon for speed and clarity. Add Rakuten when you care about points and local shops. Check Yahoo for price comparisons on specific items.

<InfoBox title="Most foreign residents misunderstand this">
  - **"Lowest listed price wins."** Shipping and points change final cost.
  - **"Rakuten is one store."** It is a marketplace of thousands of sellers.
  - **"Points are fake money."** Residents use points for real savings over years.
  - **"Amazon is always cheapest."** Often true on speed, not always on price.
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Is Amazon Japan the same as US Amazon?

Same brand, different inventory, Japanese address format, and local delivery rules.

### Do I need a Japanese credit card?

Many residents use debit or credit cards issued in Japan. Some foreign cards work on Amazon; test small purchase first.

### Is Rakuten worth it without Rakuten Card?

Yes, but points stack harder with the card ecosystem.

### What is Yahoo Shopping used for?

Marketplace shopping linked to Yahoo ID, often compared for price and used with PayPay points.

### Can I use English on Rakuten?

Limited. Browser translate helps. Amazon is easier for English.

### How do I avoid missed deliveries?

Pick a slot when you are home, use delivery box, or konbini pickup.

### Which platform is cheapest?

Varies by item and day. Compare total cost including shipping.

### Can I return items if I do not speak Japanese?

Return policies vary by seller. Amazon returns are often simpler for beginners.

### Is cash on delivery available?

Some sellers offer COD. Less common on Amazon than smaller shops.

### Should students use Amazon only?

Many students start with Amazon, then add Rakuten after first semester.

### Does Prime exist in Japan?

Yes, Amazon Prime Japan is a paid subscription with shipping benefits.

### How do points affect real savings?

Small per order, meaningful over a year if you shop online regularly.
