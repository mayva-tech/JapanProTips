## Why the guarantor system confuses foreigners

| Surprise | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Extra company on the contract** | Not just you and landlord |
| **Upfront guarantor fee** | Separate from deposit and key money |
| **Annual renewal** | Ongoing cost many countries skip |
| **Deep paperwork** | Income, employer, emergency contact |
| **Silent rejection** | Agency may not explain why |
| **No US-style credit score** | Different proof system |

Japan treats rent as a **long trust relationship**, not only a deposit hold.

## What a guarantor actually is

A **guarantor** (保証人, hoshonin) backs your lease. If you miss rent or damage bills, the landlord can claim from the guarantor first.

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Tenant (you)** | Pay rent and follow house rules |
| **Landlord / owner** | Provides unit, handles building |
| **Guarantor** | Pays if you default after legal process |
| **Agency** | Screening, paperwork, handover |

The guarantor is **not** your roommate and **not** your employer.

## Traditional guarantors vs guarantor companies

| | **Personal guarantor** | **Guarantor company (保証会社)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who** | Japanese family, sometimes employer | Licensed hosho gaisha |
| **Common for foreigners** | Rare without local family | Standard path |
| **Fee** | Often none to family | Initial + annual |
| **Approval** | Family credit and income | Company rules |
| **Examples** | Parent in Japan | Casa, GTN, Orient, etc. |

Most foreign residents use a **company named in the listing** or chosen by the agency.

## Why landlords require guarantors in Japan

| Reason | Plain language |
| --- | --- |
| **Low eviction speed** | Recovering unpaid rent takes time |
| **Aging owners** | Want third party with collection staff |
| **Agency standard** | Templates always include guarantor |
| **Damage and utilities** | Guarantor may cover move-out shortfalls |
| **Foreign tenant risk perception** | Unfair but still shapes screening |

The guarantor is **insurance for the landlord**, not a judgment on you as a person.

## How guarantor companies work

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | You apply to rent a unit |
| 2 | Agency sends documents to guarantor company |
| 3 | Company scores income, visa, stability |
| 4 | Approval or denial (sometimes conditional) |
| 5 | You sign **lease + guarantor contract** |
| 6 | You pay initial guarantor fee |
| 7 | Company remains on contract until move-out |

Some companies offer **English support** for foreign tenants. Terms still bind in Japanese.

## Typical fees and renewal costs

Fees vary by company, city, and rent amount. Illustrative **Tokyo ranges**:

| Fee type | Typical range | When paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Initial guarantor fee** | 50%-100% of monthly rent | Move-in |
| **Annual renewal** | ¥10,000-¥20,000/year or 10,000-15% of rent | Each contract year |
| **Renewal on lease extension** | Similar to annual | Lease renewal |
| **Emergency support fee** | Sometimes bundled | Move-in |

**Example: rent ¥90,000/month**

| Item | Illustrative cost |
| --- | --- |
| Initial guarantor (80%) | ¥72,000 |
| First year renewal | ¥15,000 |
| Total guarantor year 1 | ~¥87,000 |

This is **on top of** deposit, key money, agency fee, and fire insurance.

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents panic about this">
  **Guarantor fee is not deposit.** You do not get it back at move-out like shikikin. Budget it as service cost.
</InfoBox>

## Foreign resident approval realities

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid visa length** | Short visas harder |
| **Stable employment** | Full-time stronger than fresh baito |
| **Income rule** | Often 2.5-3x rent gross |
| **Japanese ability** | Helps with landlord-facing units |
| **Prior Japan address** | Second lease easier |
| **Company HR letter** | Very helpful |

**Scenario A:** Engineer, 3-year visa, ¥6M salary, rent ¥95k. Strong approval at foreign-friendly agency.

**Scenario B:** Language student, 1-year visa, part-time ¥120k/month, rent ¥70k. May need higher deposit, different guarantor company, or share house.

Denial is common. **Apply through agencies used to foreigners** rather than random suumo listings alone.

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Residence card** | Front and back copy |
| **Passport** | Sometimes |
| **Income certificate (源泉徴収票)** | From employer |
| **Employment contract** | Dates and salary |
| **Bank balance** | If income thin |
| **Student ID + part-time permit** | Students |

| **Emergency contact form** | Japan or home country |

Have PDFs ready before viewings. Delays lose units.

## Employment and income checks

| Applicant type | What guarantor checks |
| --- | --- |
| **Company employee** | Hire date, base salary, contract type |
| **Contractor** | Contract length, client stability |
| **Self-employed** | Tax records, bank inflow |
| **Student** | Allowance proof, part-time hours cap |
| **Spouse visa** | Partner income sometimes |

**Rent to income rule (typical):**

| Monthly rent | Minimum gross income (3x rule) |
| --- | --- |
| ¥70,000 | ¥210,000 |
| ¥90,000 | ¥270,000 |
| ¥120,000 | ¥360,000 |

Rules flex for corporate leases or high deposits.

## Emergency contact vs guarantor explained

| | **Emergency contact (緊急連絡先)** | **Guarantor (保証人)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Reach someone if you are ill or missing | Pay rent if you default |
| **Legal liability** | Not usually financial | Contractual payment duty |
| **Who** | Friend, family abroad | Guarantor company |
| **Language** | Any | Company handles Japanese landlord |

Do not list the same person unless they understand **both** roles.

## Why apartments sometimes reject foreigners

| Stated or unstated reason | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Building rule** | Owner banned non-Japanese tenants |
| **Language** | Landlord fears communication |
| **Visa length** | Fear of early departure |
| **Noise stereotypes** | Unfair bias |
| **Guarantor company policy** | Company rejects visa type |
| **Competition** | Japanese applicant with lower risk profile chosen |

Rejection is not always about you. **Change agency or building**, not only your personality.

## What happens if rent is unpaid

| Stage | Typical flow |
| --- | --- |
| Month 1 late | Reminder from management |
| Continued late | Guarantor company contacted |
| Guarantor pays landlord | Company pursues you for reimbursement |
| Serious default | Legal collection, visa issues indirect |
| Move-out debt | Cleaning, repair, unpaid utilities added |

Guarantor companies have **call centers** landlords prefer over chasing individuals.

## Foreign-friendly real estate agencies

| Approach | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| **Foreigner-focused agencies** | Explain guarantor in English |
| **Corporate relocation** | Employer guarantor sometimes |
| **Share houses** | Lower screening, less privacy |
| **UR / public housing** | Different rules, long waits |

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Treating guarantor fee as refundable deposit
- Applying without **income proof** ready
- Using emergency contact who does not answer Japanese phone
- Chasing listings that say **外国人不可**
- Hiding prior lease debt in Japan
- Signing guarantor contract without translation review
- Missing **renewal payment** and losing guarantor coverage

## FAQ

### Can I rent without a guarantor?

Rare. Some corporate housing, share houses, or UR paths differ. Standard private lease: almost always needs one.

### Can my employer be guarantor?

Some large employers do. Ask HR. Most use external companies instead.

### Does guarantor fee count toward deposit?

No. Different line items on the invoice.

### What if guarantor company rejects me?

Try another company via different agency or lower rent bracket.

### Is guarantor the same as fire insurance?

No. Fire insurance (火災保険) is separate mandatory policy.

### Can family abroad be guarantor?

Uncommon for standard leases. Companies want Japan-based collection paths.

### Do I pay renewal if I move out after 10 months?

Read contract. Some prorate, some charge full year.

### Will unpaid rent affect visa renewal?

Not automatic, but serious debt and legal trouble can hurt applications.

### Can students use guarantor companies?

Yes, with student plans and often higher fees or co-signer rules.

### Should I negotiate guarantor fees?

Sometimes on renewal. Initial fee negotiation is limited.
