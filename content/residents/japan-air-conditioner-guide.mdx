## Why air conditioners in Japan confuse foreigners

| Surprise | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Wall unit does heat and cool** | Heat pump common |
| **Remote has 20 buttons** | Many unused daily |
| **Dry mode is not cool mode** | Dehumidify focus |
| **No central HVAC** | Room by room |
| **Filter cleaning is your job** | Not building staff |

Japan apartments often ship with **one wall-mounted ルームエアコン (room AC)** per main room.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Treating the unit like a hotel thermostat with one "cold" dial. Japanese units are **mode-based systems** with seasonal logic.

**Scenario:** July move-in. You press random buttons. Room feels damp at 25°C. You learn **冷房 (cool)** vs **ドライ (dry)** and sleep finally improves.

## AC units as both cooling and heating systems

| Mode (common label) | Function |
| --- | --- |
| **冷房 / クール** | Cooling |
| **暖房 / ヒート** | Heating (heat pump) |
| **ドライ** | Dehumidify, light cooling feel |
| **送風** | Fan only |
| **自動** | Auto switch by sensor |

| Apartment type | Typical setup |
| --- | --- |
| **1K** | One unit in main room |
| **1LDK** | Living plus sometimes bedroom |
| **Older building** | Weaker heating in cold zones |

Heating is **not gas central air** in most urban rentals. The wall unit is primary.

| **入タイマー** | On timer |

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Changing temperature without checking **mode icon on remote screen**. You may be in heat while trying to cool.

## Cooling vs dry mode differences

| Mode | Best when | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cool (冷房)** | Hot days, you want lower temp | Strong cooling |
| **Dry (ドライ)** | Humid, sticky, 26-30°C outside | Less cold, less damp |
| **Fan only** | Mild days, circulate air | No temp change much |

| Humidity scenario | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Rainy week, clothes not drying | Dry mode plus dehumidifier |
| 35°C heat wave | Cool 26-28°C plus fan |
| Night sleep | Cool or dry, fan low, louver up |

| --- | --- |

| --- | --- |
| **1K Tokyo, AC most evenings July** | Many residents report noticeable jump vs May |
| **Heat mode January nights** | Can rival mild summer if old unit |
| **Fan only** | Small cost |

| Bill driver | Impact |
| --- | --- |

## FAQ

### Do all apartments include air conditioning?

Most urban rentals include at least one wall unit. Confirm before signing lease.

### What temperature should I set in summer?

Many residents use 26-28°C with fan. Adjust for health and bill.

### Is dry mode cheaper than cool mode?

Often yes. It runs less aggressive cooling.

### Can AC heat replace kotatsu?

Sometimes in mild winter. Many use both.

### Why does my AC drip water inside?

Drain line may be clogged. Clean or call maintenance.

### How often clean the filter?

Every 2-4 weeks in heavy season, monthly minimum.

### Will AC make my electricity bill huge?

Heavy use in summer can. Timers and higher set temp help.

### What if remote is only Japanese?

Search unit model plus 取扱説明書 PDF. Photo translate buttons.

### Is leaving AC on all day bad?

Costly. Timer or on-before-home pattern common.

### Who fixes a broken unit?

Report to landlord or management. Do not DIY refrigerant.

### Does heat mode work in Hokkaido?

Colder regions may need extra heating. Climate varies.
