## Why frozen food culture in Japan surprises foreigners

| Abroad assumption | Japan resident reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Frozen means low quality** | Wide mid-range selection |
| **Microwave is snack only** | Main dinner tool |
| **Meal prep Sunday** | Buy frozen weekday |
| **Large freezer** | Compact drawer |
| **Takeout default** | Konbini plus freezer mix |

Frozen aisles are **engineered for single households**.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Thinking Japanese food culture is all fresh fish daily. **Frozen udon, rice, and bento components** are mainstream for workers and students.

## FAQ

### Is frozen food common for Japanese residents?

Yes. **Singles, workers, parents** all use supermarket freezer aisles.

### Do I need a microwave in my apartment?

Most units include or allow one. **Check lease**. Life is harder without it.

### Can I eat konbini food every day?

Many do short term. **Balance** with veg and cooking when you can.

### How long can I keep frozen food?

Follow package **best-by (消費期限)**. Freezer stops spoilage, quality fades.

### What wattage is my microwave?

Sticker inside door. Match package line.

### Are retort pouches the same as frozen?

**Retort (レトト)** is shelf stable pouch, boil or microwave. Different aisle.

### Can I microwave bento in plastic tray?

If label says **レンジ可**. When unsure, transfer to plate.
