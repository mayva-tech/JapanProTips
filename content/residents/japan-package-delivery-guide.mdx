## Quick answer: Why package delivery matters so much in Japan

**Japan runs on parcels.** Groceries, furniture, Mercari sales, and Amazon orders all use the same carrier habits: time windows, missed slips, and self-service redelivery.

| If you ignore delivery culture | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Miss slip twice | Parcel may return to sender |
| Wrong intercom name | Repeated failed attempts |
| Tiny mailbox only | Slips hide inside, easy to miss |
| No redelivery booked | Package sits at depot, then returns |

**Quick answer:** Scan the QR on the slip the same evening, pick a time slot you will actually be home, and fix your intercom name once. Most stress drops after the first month.

## Missed delivery slips explained

**不在票 (fuzaihyo)** is left when nobody accepts the parcel.

| Slip section | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Tracking number | Track on carrier site |
| QR code | Schedule redelivery (easiest) |
| Phone number | Automated redelivery line (Japanese) |
| Date attempted | Reference |
| Depot location | Temporary hold if listed |

| Common miss reason | What actually happened |
| --- | --- |
| At work during slot | Normal slip |
| Intercom name mismatch | Driver cannot confirm unit |
| No answer in ~2 minutes | Driver moves to next stop |
| Building gate locked | Slip or depot hold |
| Buzzer confusion | Wrong button, no pickup |

### Realistic missed-delivery anxiety

The slip is not a fine. It is a prompt. Most residents feel relief after their first successful QR redelivery at 11 p.m. on the couch.

| First-time fear | Reality |

| "I lost my package" | It is usually at a local depot |
| "My Japanese is not good enough" | QR flow needs little Japanese |

## FAQ

### What is a missed delivery slip in Japan?

A notice (不在票) left when you were not available. Use the QR or tracking number to schedule redelivery or pickup.

### How do I schedule redelivery?

Scan the QR on the slip, or use the carrier website with the tracking number. Same evening is fine.

### What if I miss delivery twice?

The parcel may return to sender. Contact the seller quickly.

### Can I pick up at a convenience store?

Yes, if you selected konbini hold at checkout or redirected from the carrier app.

### Is Yamato the same as Kuroneko?

Yes. Yamato Transport uses the black cat logo.

### Do I need Japanese for redelivery?

QR flows need minimal Japanese. Phone IVR is harder.

### Why did delivery fail if I was home?

Wrong intercom name, slow answer, or gate code missing are common causes.

### How long will the depot hold my package?

Often about one week. Check your slip.

### Are delivery lockers free?

Usually free within the pickup window. Late fees may apply.

### What is okihai (置き配)?

Authorized leave-at-location delivery. Register with carrier and confirm building rules.

### Can neighbors accept my package?

Sometimes, if arranged. Building rules vary.

### Are fake delivery texts common?

Yes. Open tracking only in official apps. See [phone scams guide](/residents/japan-phone-scams-fraud-guide).

### Does Amazon Japan use Yamato?

Often yes in cities. Locker and counter options are common.
