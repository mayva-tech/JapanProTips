## FAQ

### Do I need Japanese to use local shops?

Basic phrases help. **Pointing and numbers** work for many purchases.

### Are local shops cheaper?

Sometimes for produce and bread. **Supermarkets win** on bulk and sales.

### Is cash required?

Often at small stands. **Carry coins**.

### How do I become a regular?

Visit the same shop **consistently**, polite brief thanks.

### Are unattended vegetable stands honest?

Mostly yes in stable neighborhoods. **Pay listed price**.

### Do local shops hate foreigners?

Most do not care if you **follow flow**: queue, pay, leave.

### What if a shop has no price tag?

Ask いくらですか or look for sign.
