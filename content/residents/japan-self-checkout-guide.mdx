## Why daily machines in Japan feel overwhelming at first

| Factor | First-week stress |
| --- | --- |
| **Many steps per machine** | Scan, bag, pay, receipt |
| **Kanji-heavy menus** | English not always available |
| **Multiple payment types** | Cash, IC, QR, credit |
| **Social pressure** | Line behind you |
| **Different UI per chain** | No single national standard |
| **Staff assist without words** | Fast gesture help |

Machines are **repeatable systems**, not one-off puzzles.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Thinking one bad experience means Japan is anti-foreigner at checkout. Usually it is **unfamiliar UI plus a queue**, not personal.

Grocery context: [grocery shopping in Japan](/residents/japan-grocery-shopping-guide). Payments: [mobile payments](/residents/japan-mobile-payment-guide).

## Grocery self-checkout systems explained

| Step | Typical action |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | Tap screen to start (レジ, self register) |
| **2** | Scan barcoded items |
| **3** | Weigh or select produce |
| **4** | Tap member card if any |
| **5** | Choose payment |
| **6** | Bag at side table |
| **7** | Take receipt |

| Store type | Self-checkout note |
| --- | --- |
| **AEON, Ito-Yokado, Life** | Mixed staff lanes plus self |
| **OK, Gyomu Super** | Often busy self lanes |
| **Premium supermarkets** | More staff service default |

**Workflow example:** Sunday 6 p.m. at Life supermarket. You choose セルフ (self). Scan six items. One apple needs produce lookup. Staff taps 青りんご for you. You pay with Suica. Total 1,240 yen. Bag at side counter.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Skipping produce registration. **Unbarcoded fruit** must be entered or weighed, or the exit alarm may sound.

## Cash vs IC card vs QR payment workflows

| Method | Where common |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash** | Still accepted almost everywhere |
| **Transport IC (Suica, PASMO)** | Supermarkets, konbini, many chains |
| **Credit tap** | Urban stores, higher totals |
| **PayPay, d Barai, etc.** | QR on screen, scan with phone |
| **Store point apps** | Rakuten, Ponta linked |

IC guide: [Suica and PASMO](/guides/suica-pasmo-guide). Resident IC: [IC card Japan](/residents/ic-card-japan). Money basics: [money and payments](/guides/money-payments-japan).

| Payment tip | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Hold IC flat on reader** | Until beep |
| **QR: brightness up** | Scanner fails in dim |
| **Split pay rare at self** | Prepare one method |

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Tapping IC before the total is finalized. Wait for **合計 (total)** screen.

## FAQ

### Can I use Suica at supermarket self-checkout?

Usually yes. Look for **交通系IC** on the payment screen.

### What if I cannot read the screen?

Use **staff register**, point at basket, or ask 英語はありますか. Many areas help.

### Do I need a Japanese phone for QR pay?

You need a **Japanese or compatible wallet app** setup. See mobile payment guide.

### Why did the machine beep loudly?

Usually **unscanned item** or payment error. Staff button or 店員呼出.

### Are restaurant ticket machines cash only?

Many take **IC and coins**. Some take bills. Check icons before ordering.

### Is self-checkout cheaper?

No. Same prices. Sometimes **shorter lines**.

### Can tourists and residents use the same machines?

Yes. Resident difference is **frequency and point cards**, not access.

### What about tax-free tourist checkout?

Separate counter rules. Residents pay **standard tax** at daily shops.
