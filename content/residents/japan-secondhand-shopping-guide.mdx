## Why secondhand shopping in Japan surprises foreigners

| Surprise | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **High cleanliness** | Items washed, tagged, graded |
| **Testing stations** | Game consoles and amps checked |
| **No haggling in chains** | Fixed price tags |
| **Fast online deals** | Mercari and Jimoty sell in hours |
| **Disposal cost on exit** | You own the discard fee later |
| **Tiny store parking** | Plan carry or delivery |

Used does not mean broken. It often means **someone moved**.

## The recycle shop culture explained

**Recycle shop** (リサイクルショップ) in Japan means **resale store**, not municipal recycling center.

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy used** | Save money, reduce waste |
| **Sell used** | Store buys or consignment |
| **Grade labels** | S, A, B condition tiers |
| **Receipt** | Proof for warranty disputes |
| **No returns** | Common on used goods |

Culture respects **mottainai** (waste not). Good items get second life.

## Popular secondhand chains

### Hard Off

Electronics, instruments, cameras, tools. Staff often power-test gear.

### Book Off

Books, games, DVDs, manga. Buyback counters at many locations.

### Hobby Off

Models, figures, craft supplies. Niche but organized.

### 2nd Street

Fashion and home goods. Large clothing selection.

### Treasure Factory

Furniture, appliances, sports gear. Warehouse feel.

| Chain | Best for | Price level | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hard Off | Audio, PC parts, games | Mid-low | Minimal |
| Book Off | Media | Very low | Minimal |
| 2nd Street | Clothes, bags | Low-mid | Some tags |
| Treasure Factory | Sofa, tables, bikes | Mid | Minimal |
| Mode Off (related) | Fashion | Low | Minimal |

## Online secondhand marketplaces

### Mercari

Japan's largest C2C app. Escrow payment, shipping labels, reviews.

| Tip | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Use Japanese keywords | Translate item name |
| Check seller rating | 95%+ with history |
| Read shipping cost | Who pays |
| Avoid too-good price | Scam risk |

### Jimoty

Local pickup, often free or cash. Great for **furniture you carry**.

| Tip | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Refresh often | Deals vanish fast |
| Message in Japanese | Template helps |
| Meet in public | Safety |
| Bring exact cash | Common |

### Yahoo Auctions

Auction format, older user base. Good for **collectibles and bulk lots**.

## Recycling laws and disposal culture

You cannot leave furniture on the street.

| When leaving Japan | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Sell on Jimoty | Recover cash |
| Recycle shop buyback | Small return |
| Sodai gomi fee | Pay for oversized pickup |
| Move-out service | Company hauls all |

When you leave Japan, plan resale or disposal before move-out day. Utilities: [utilities guide](/residents/japan-utilities-guide).

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Buying sofa before **measuring door**
- Skipping **plug-in test** at store
- Forgetting **disposal cost** on exit
- Mercari purchase without **size check** (Japanese cm labels)
- Leaving items in apartment at move-out (illegal dumping risk)
- No **yen cash** for Jimoty pickup
- Buying mattress used to save ¥20,000

## FAQ

### Is secondhand shopping common for Japanese people?

Yes. Students, expats, and families all use recycle shops.

### Can I sell my stuff when I leave Japan?

Yes. Jimoty, Mercari, Book Off buyback, or recycle shop.

### Does Mercari work in English?

App has English mode. Listings are mostly Japanese.

### Are used washers safe?

Chain-tested units are reasonable. Private sale: run test cycle on site.

### Do I need a Japanese phone for Mercari?

Japanese number helps verification.

### Can I use credit card at Hard Off?

Often yes. Jimoty pickup is usually cash.

### What is sodai gomi?

Oversized garbage fee system for furniture disposal.

### Is Yahoo Auctions still worth it?

Yes for hobby gear and bulk lots if you read Japanese.

### Can I return used items?

Usually no at recycle chains. Mercari has dispute window for fraud.

### Best city for thrift?

Tokyo and Osaka have the most volume. Regional cities have fewer options.
