## Quick answer: Why Japan winter surprises foreign residents

**Outdoor winter in Tokyo or Osaka is often mild compared to Canada or northern Europe. Indoor winter can still feel harsh** because many apartments are built for humid summers, not insulated winters.

| Surprise | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Cold mornings indoors | Thin walls, little central heating |
| Dry air | Heating removes humidity |
| High electricity use | Heat via AC or space heaters |
| Slow laundry drying | Less sun, no dryer |
| Dark early evenings | Mood shift is real |

**Quick answer:** Plan heating method, humidity, and layering before January. Winter comfort is a system, not one purchase.

## Apartment heating realities in Japan

| Reality | What residents do |
| --- | --- |
| No central heating in most rentals | Room-by-room heat |
| AC unit does heat and cool | Primary heater in many units |
| Hallway and toilet stay cold | Close doors, use slippers |
| Old buildings leak air | Curtains, door draft stoppers |

## Why indoors sometimes feel colder than expected

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Single-pane or thin windows | Heat escapes fast |
| Concrete construction | Walls feel cold to touch |
| Unheated genkan | Cold air enters at entry |
| Open plan 1LDK | One warm room, rest cool |

You may wear a jacket inside the first month. That is common, not failure.

## Air conditioner heating confusion

| Topic | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Remote labels** | Japanese buttons; learn heat mode |
| **Set temperature** | 20-22°C is common target |
| **Fan direction** | Point airflow down in heat mode |
| **Filter cleaning** | Dirty filter weakens heat |
| **Dry mode vs heat** | Different buttons; do not mix up |

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Never switching to heat mode | Read manual once |
| Heating whole 1LDK with one unit | Heat main room only |
| Leaving windows cracked | Close while heating |

## Dry air and winter discomfort

| Symptom | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| Dry throat | Low indoor humidity |
| Static shocks | Dry air |
| Cracked skin | Heating plus low humidity |
| Waking thirsty | Overnight dry air |

Tokyo winter outdoor air can feel damp, but **heated indoor air often feels dry**.

## Humidifiers and why residents suddenly buy one

| Humidifier type | Apartment note |
| --- | --- |
| **Ultrasonic** | Quiet, needs cleaning |
| **Steam** | Stronger, higher power use |
| **Hybrid with air purifier** | Popular in cities |

Target roughly 40-50% humidity if comfortable. Too high risks mold in summer mindset; winter needs balance.

Seasonal products: [seasonal home products](/residents/japan-seasonal-home-products-guide).

<InfoBox title="Most foreign residents misunderstand this">
  - **"Japan winter is humid everywhere indoors."** Heated rooms are often dry.
  - **"If I heat one room, the whole apartment is fine."** Hall, bath, and kitchen may stay cold.
  - **"AC heating is optional."** For many units, it is the main heater.
  - **"Winter bills are like summer bills."** Heating can cost more than cooling for some homes.
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Is Japan winter very cold?

Depends on region. Tokyo winters are mild outdoors but cold indoors in many apartments.

### Why is my apartment colder than outside sometimes?

Poor insulation and no heating overnight. Morning is coldest.

### Should I use AC for heating?

Yes in most modern rentals. It is the standard primary heater.

### Do I need a humidifier?

Many residents buy one for heated dry air.

### How much do electricity bills rise?

Varies. Heating use can add thousands of yen monthly in cold months.

### Are kotatsu worth it?

Popular for living rooms. Great comfort per yen if you sit at table often.

### How do I dry laundry in winter?

Indoor rack, bathroom dry mode, or laundromat dryer.

### What is HeatTech?

Thermal innerwear sold widely at Uniqlo and others.

### Is window condensation bad?

Common. Wipe and ventilate briefly to reduce mold risk.

### Do foreigners get winter depression in Japan?

Some do from shorter daylight. Light, routine, and social plans help.

### What region is coldest for residents?

Hokkaido and northern Tohoku are coldest. Okinawa is mild.

### Should I buy an electric blanket?

Many residents do for sleep comfort.
