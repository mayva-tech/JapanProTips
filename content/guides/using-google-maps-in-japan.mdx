## Quick answer: Is Google Maps reliable in Japan?

**Yes for most urban travel**, especially trains, walking, and restaurants. It is **not perfect** underground, at mega-stations, or inside multi-floor buildings.

| Trust Maps for | Double-check manually |
| --- | --- |
| Train routes and times | Exit number for your hotel |
| Walking in open streets | Underground transfer paths |
| Restaurant pins (rough) | Exact shop inside building |
| Bus in cities | Rural bus timetables |

Train basics: [how trains work in Japan](/guides/japan-trains). Common errors: [train mistakes](/guides/japan-train-mistakes).

## Why Google Maps feels different in Japan

| Japan factor | Map effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Mega-stations** | Route time includes indoor walking |
| **Many exits** | Wrong exit adds blocks |
| **Dense buildings** | GPS drift, pin on wrong side |
| **Vertical malls** | Floor number matters |
| **Excellent transit data** | Maps love complex transfers |
| **English signage** | Good in cities, not universal |

You are navigating **3D cities** with a **2D arrow**.

## Understanding Japanese train stations

Stations are not just platforms. They are **shopping, food, and tunnel systems**.

| Station layer | Tourist experience |
| --- | --- |
| Street level | Exits, taxis, rain |
| Ticket gates | IC tap in/out |
| Platforms | Train boarding |
| Underground malls | Long corridors between lines |
| Mid-level concourses | Sign forests |

**Quick answer:** Your train arrived on time. You can still be **late to dinner** because the station ate 20 minutes.

<OperationalWarning noteId="gmaps-station-time-budget">
  <p>
    Maps love a tight transfer that assumes you already know which ticket gate
    family you need. Add a few minutes the first time you enter a mega hub, even
    when the line color looks obvious on screen.
  </p>
</OperationalWarning>

## Why station exits matter so much

Tokyo exits are numbered (**East Exit, Exit 3, etc.**). Hotels and sights often say **"use Exit X"**.

| Wrong exit | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Opposite side of block | 10-15 min walk |
| Wrong street for taxi | Driver confusion |
| Extra stairs with luggage | Energy + time |

Before leaving a station, check:

1. Line color and direction on signs
2. **Exit number** toward your destination
3. Whether you need **underground passage** vs street

## The underground navigation problem

Maps show a line between points. In Japan, that line may run **through a basement city**.

| Underground issue | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| GPS loss | Blue dot freezes or jumps |
| Long passages | You follow crowds wrong way |
| Multiple operators | JR vs subway gates |
| Shopping detours | You exit into mall, not street |

**Example:** Shinjuku transfer. Map says 5 minutes. You pass bakeries, pharmacies, and three staircases. You arrive sweaty. The train was easy. The station was the boss.

<RealityCheck noteId="gmaps-underground-gps">
  <p>
    Under concrete, the blue dot lies with confidence. When GPS drifts, switch to
    reading ceiling signs and gate numbers like it is 1998 on purpose. The map
    catches up when you surface.
  </p>
</RealityCheck>

## Walking times are often underestimated

Google walking times assume **steady outdoor pace**. Japan walking includes:

| Hidden time | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Traffic lights | Frequent stops |
| Crowds | Shinjuku, Shibuya slowdown |
| Stairs | Stations lack elevators sometimes |
| Wrong sidewalk side | River barriers, blocks |
| Photo stops | You will stop |

Add **25-40% buffer** for first-time walks in busy areas.

Fatigue stacks: [Japan travel fatigue](/guides/japan-travel-fatigue).

## Multi-level stations and confusion

| Station type | Confusion pattern |
| --- | --- |
| **JR + subway hub** | Different ticket gates |
| **Department store attached** | You exit into mall |
| **Multiple lines same name** | Platform wrong direction |
| **Express vs local** | Right platform, wrong train |

<InfoBox title="Most tourists misunderstand this">
  - **"Maps will tell me the right exit."** Sometimes yes. Often you must read **station signs**.
  - **"Short transfer time means easy."** Short can mean **fast walk**, not easy walk.
  - **"I'll follow the crowd."** Crowds go home, not to your hotel.
  - **"GPS is enough."** Underground, signs beat GPS.
</InfoBox>

## Building names vs street addresses

Japan addresses are **block-based**. Shops use **building names**.

| Map shows | You need |
| --- | --- |
| Pin on street | Building entrance |
| "1-2-3" address | Shop name on directory board |
| Mall pin | Floor and zone |

**Restaurant tactic:** Search shop name + **building name**. Screenshot the **entrance photo** if available.

## Train navigation comparison

| Situation | What tourists expect | Reality in Japan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Station transfer** | Quick platform hop | Long underground walk, signs critical |
| **Walking between exits** | Short outdoor stroll | 10+ min through station interior |
| **Platform changes** | Same hall | Different level, new gate |
| **Underground navigation** | GPS guides smoothly | Follow signs; screenshot route |

## Google Maps and train transfer strategy

| Tip | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Tap route **details** | See line names and transfer minutes |
| Expand **steps** before moving | Read while on Wi-Fi |
| Compare **1-transfer vs 2-transfer** | Sometimes +6 min saves stress |
| Check **last train** separately at night | Maps can miss nuance |
| Use IC card | Faster gates ([Suica/Pasmo](/guides/suica-pasmo-guide)) |

**Quick answer:** The fastest route is not always the **lowest stress** route.

## FAQ

### Is Google Maps accurate in Japan?

Generally yes for trains and walking in cities. Underground and building interiors are weaker.

### Does Google Maps work with Japan trains?

Yes for most JR and major subway routing. Always confirm platform and direction on signs.

### Why does Google Maps send me through weird station paths?

Mega-stations have many valid paths. Maps optimizes time, not comfort.

### Which station exit should I use?

Use the exit closest to your destination. Hotel and blogs often specify exit numbers.

### Do I need offline maps?

Recommended for backup. Screenshot key routes too.

### Is Apple Maps okay in Japan?

Usable in cities. Many travelers still prefer Google for transit breadth.

### Why am I lost even with GPS?

You may be underground, in a mall, or on the wrong side of a block.

### How early should I arrive at the station?

Add **10-15 minutes** beyond map time for big stations and ticket gates.

### Can I navigate Japan without Japanese?

Yes in major cities with Maps and Translate. Rural areas need more planning.

### What if I miss my train?

Maps will replan. Watch whether next train is local or express.
