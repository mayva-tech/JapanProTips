## Why bicycles are such a big part of daily life in Japan

| Reason | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Dense stations** | Last mile by bike |
| **Flat river paths** | Easy routes in cities |
| **Low car ownership** in cities | Bike fills gap |
| **School culture** | Kids cycle early |
| **Parking at stations** | Commuter habit |
| **Short errands** | Konbini runs |

A bike turns a **45 minute walk** into a **12 minute ride** for many residents.

## Mamachari vs sports bikes vs electric bikes

| Type | Best for | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mamachari (ママチャリ)** | Daily errands, commute | Basket, fenders, sturdy |
| **Sports / road bike** | Fitness, speed | Less basket, theft target |
| **Electric assist (電動アシスト)** | Hills, long commute | Heavier, register as bicycle |
| **Folding** | Train plus bike combo | Small storage |

| Comparison | Mamachari | E-bike | Sports |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost used | ¥5,000-¥15,000 | ¥30,000-¥80,000+ | ¥20,000+ |
| Rain ready | Usually yes | Often yes | Varies |
| Theft risk | Medium | High | High |
| Apartment storage | OK | Heavy | OK if fold |

**Scenario:** Commute 1.5 km to station daily. Used mamachari ¥8,000 plus ¥2,000/month coin parking. Cheaper than extra train stop fare over a year.

## Bicycle registration explained

**防犯登録 (bohan toroku)** links your bike to your address.

| Step | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Buy bike | Shop registers often |
| Bring bike and ID | Police or shop |
| Pay small fee | About ¥500-¥1,000 |
| Sticker on frame | Proof |

| Why register | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Police return stolen bikes | If recovered |
| Proof of ownership | Avoid "abandoned bike" removal |
| Required at some parking | Monthly lots check |

Legal details: [bicycle rules in Japan](/residents/japan-bike-rules).

## Bike theft realities

Theft happens. **Lock always**, even for five minutes.

| Lock level | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Ring lock on rear wheel** | Basic, weak alone |
| **U-lock or chain** | Station parking |
| **Two locks** | Overnight |
| **Register serial** | Recovery chance |

| Target | Risk |
| --- | --- |
| Expensive e-bike | High |
| Old mamachari | Lower but still stolen |
| Unlocked at konbini | Very high |

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents misunderstand this">
  **"Nobody steals old bikes"** is false. Thieves want any rideable bike or parts.
</InfoBox>

Rain does not stop Japan. **Gear does the work**.

| Gear | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Fenders** | Standard on mamachari |
| **Rain cape (ポンチョ)** | Covers handlebars |
| **Waterproof pants** | Legs dry |
| **Bag cover** | Groceries dry |
| **Lights** | Required at night anyway |

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Riding **without lights** at dusk
- **Double parking** at station rush
- **No registration** on used buy
- **One weak lock** at supermarket
- **Riding drunk** after nomikai
- **Blocking building entrance** with bike
- **Sports bike on crowded sidewalk** at speed
- Ignoring **no bicycle** signs on busy streets

## FAQ

### Do I need a license for a bicycle?

Standard pedal bike no. Some e-bike classes have rules. Check label.

### Can I ride on the sidewalk?

Often yes slowly where signed. Follow local rules.

### How do I register a used bike?

Police registration desk with ID and bike.

### Is bike commuting cheaper than train?

Sometimes similar. Saves time on last mile.

### What lock should I buy?

U-lock plus ring lock for stations.

### Can I take a bike on the train?

Regular bikes usually no. Folding sometimes off-peak.

### Are e-bikes worth it for hills?

Yes in hilly cities like parts of Kobe or Sendai.

### What if my bike is impounded?

Pay fee at city office, bring ID.

### Is insurance mandatory?

Depends on city. Cheap and recommended.

### Can kids ride to school alone?

Local culture yes at age varies. Your comfort matters.
