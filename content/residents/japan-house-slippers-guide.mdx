## Why indoor footwear culture feels unfamiliar at first

| Abroad habit | Japan common pattern |
| --- | --- |
| **Shoes through the house** | Shoes stop at genkan |
| **One slipper type** | House vs bathroom split |
| **Bare feet indoors** | Socks or slippers preferred |
| **Outdoor shoes in bedroom** | Rare |
| **Guest keeps shoes on** | Usually removes at door |

The system is about **clean floors in small spaces**, not performance.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Treating every slipper rule as a strict test foreigners always fail. Most neighbors care about **wet mud and bathroom slippers on tatami**, not perfection on day three.

## The genkan entrance system explained

| Zone | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Outside step** | Street shoes |
| **Genkan floor (下駄箱 area)** | Shoe removal only |
| **Step up (一段上がり)** | Slippers or socks begin |
| **Upper floor / main room** | No street shoes ever |

| Genkan item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Shoe cabinet (下駄箱)** | Store outdoor pairs |
| **Umbrella stand** | Drip control |
| **Small shelf** | Keys, mail |
| **Slippers on floor** | Quick entry |

**Entry scenario:** You return from rain. Shoes on genkan tile. Shake umbrella in stand. Step up in slippers. Wet shoes never touch the wood or vinyl beyond the step.

## Bathroom slippers and hygiene separation

| Rule | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| **Bathroom slippers stay in bath area** | Toilet and shower moisture |
| **Switch at toilet door** | Most common layout |
| **Never wear bath slippers to kitchen** | Hygiene split |

| Layout | Pattern |
| --- | --- |
| **Unit bath apartment** | One pair inside toilet room |
| **Separate toilet room** | Slippers hung or floor by door |

## FAQ

### Do I always need slippers in Japanese apartments?

Not legally. Most residents use **slippers or socks** past genkan for comfort and cleanliness.

### Can I wear socks only?

Yes in many homes. **Clean socks** on tatami. Some hosts prefer slippers.

### What if my apartment has no genkan step?

Use the **floor change** as boundary: tile or lower area for shoes, raised area for indoor feet.

### Are bathroom slippers mandatory?

Strong social norm. **Do not mix** with kitchen or living areas.

### What about shared houses?

House rules vary. Read the **posted genkan chart**.

### Do workplaces require indoor shoes?

Some do. Schools almost always require **indoor shoes (上履き)**.

### Can I ask guests to remove shoes?

Yes. Normal. Offer slippers or say socks OK.

### Where to buy cheap slippers?

**100-yen shops**, drugstores, home centers.
