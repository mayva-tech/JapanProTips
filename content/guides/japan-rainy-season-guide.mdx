## What Japan's rainy season actually is

**Tsuyu** (梅雨) is the seasonal front that brings prolonged cloudy, wet weather before summer heat peaks. It is tied to the **East Asian rainy season pattern**, not random storms every day.

| Fact | Tourist takeaway |
| --- | --- |
| Not 24/7 downpour | Many days are **overcast with bursts** |
| Regional timing differs | Hokkaido often skips classic tsuyu |
| Ends into summer humidity | Rain eases; heat and stickiness rise |
| Plums ripen (*baiu*) | Cultural name, not storm name |

You are planning for **probability**, not guaranteed washout.

## When rainy season happens by region

Dates shift year to year. Use this as **planning ranges**, not exact forecasts.

| Region | Typical tsuyu window |
| --- | --- |
| **Okinawa** | Earlier: often May into June |
| **Kyushu, Shikoku** | Late May to mid/late June |
| **Kansai (Kyoto, Osaka)** | Early June to mid July |
| **Tokyo, Kanto** | Early/mid June to mid July |
| **Tohoku** | Mid June to late July |
| **Hokkaido** | Often **no classic tsuyu**; cooler, some rain |

Check forecasts 7-10 days before travel. Month guide: [Japan weather by month](/guides/japan-weather-by-month).

## What the weather really feels like

| Element | What tourists notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Temperature** | Warm, not peak summer furnace yet in June |
| **Humidity** | High; clothes feel damp |
| **Rain** | Steady light rain or sudden showers |
| **Wind** | Usually moderate; typhoons are separate season |
| **Mood** | Grey skies, green gardens, misty mountains |

**Example day:** Morning drizzle at hotel. Afternoon clears. You visit a covered shopping arcade and a museum. Evening light rain again. You dry off at a ramen shop. Trip still counts as success.

## Common misconceptions tourists have

<InfoBox title="Tourists overestimate this">
  - **"June is always raining."** Many hours are dry or only misting.
  - **"Trip is ruined."** Indoor Japan is world-class.
  - **"Umbrellas useless in wind."** Compact umbrellas + konbini culture work fine in cities.
  - **"Everything closes."** Attractions run; some gardens are **more beautiful** in rain.
  - **"Same as typhoon."** Tsuyu is usually gentler; typhoons are separate risk window.
</InfoBox>

## Is rainy season a bad time to visit Japan?

**Not automatically.** It is a tradeoff trip.

| Downside | Upside |
| --- | --- |
| Wet shoes, humidity | Fewer crowds at some sites |
| Grey photos | Lush gardens, hydrangeas |
| Plan changes | Lower hotel prices sometimes |
| Laundry slower | Comfortable museum days |

Bad time if you **only** planned outdoor hikes and open-roof views with no backup.

Good time if you mix **temples, food, museums, shopping arcades, onsen**.

## Benefits of visiting during rainy season

- **Hydrangeas** (*ajisai*) peak in June in many areas
- **Greener** temple moss and forest trails (with caution)
- **Less brutal heat** than August in many regions
- **Easier restaurant seats** than peak cherry or autumn weeks
- **Atmospheric photos** if you embrace mist and umbrellas

Kyoto and Kamakura are famous for rainy-season flowers. Hokkaido can be a **weather escape** if timing aligns.

## What to pack

| Item | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Compact travel umbrella** | Backup when konbini sells out |
| **Light waterproof jacket** | Breathable better than plastic poncho all day |
| **Quick-dry clothing** | Cotton stays wet |
| **Water-resistant shoes** or spare pair | Feet happiness |
| **Small towel** | Wipe bags and glasses |
| **Plastic zip bags** | Electronics, passport |
| **Light layers** | Cool rain, warm trains |

Full seasonal list: [Japan packing list](/guides/japan-packing-list).

**Example pack:** Uniqlo quick-dry tee, light rain shell, one casual sneaker + one sandal for hotel, foldable umbrella in daypack.

## Umbrellas and convenience store culture

Japan sells umbrellas everywhere when skies darken.

| Tip | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Konbini umbrellas** | ¥500-¥700 clear vinyl common |
| **Train station shops** | Same |
| **Leave wet umbrella** | Use store racks or plastic bags |
| **Do not block** | Shake lightly outside, not inside train |

Konbini basics: [convenience store guide](/guides/japan-convenience-store-guide).

<TipBox title="Rainy-day survival tip">
  Buy a **clear umbrella** once. It survives wind better than you expect and everyone uses them. Leave it at hotel on sunny half-days if needed.
</TipBox>

## Shoes and clothing realities

| Choice | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| **White sneakers** | Stain and stay wet |
| **Mesh running shoes** | Soak through |
| **Leather without treatment** | Sad |
| **Water-resistant walkers** | Best city bet |
| **Sandals** | OK for dry days only |
| **Long hems dragging** | Mud and puddles |

Carry **plastic bag** for wet umbrella in backpack.

## Indoor activities that become better in rain

| Activity | Why rain helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Museums** | Ueno, Tokyo National Museum, teamLab if booked |
| **Department stores** | Full floors, food halls |
| **Covered arcades** | Kyoto Sanjo, Osaka Shinsaibashi sections |
| **Aquariums** | Osaka Kaiyukan, etc. |
| **Onsen day trip** | Rain on rotenburo is cinematic |
| **Movie theaters, game centers** | Easy win |
| **Cooking or food tours** | Indoor focus |
| **Konbini food crawl** | Peak comfort food weather |

**Practical scenario:** Rain morning. TeamLab or museum slot. Lunch in basement food hall. Temple in light drizzle with umbrella afternoon if forecast improves.

## Train delays and transportation considerations

Tsuyu rain **usually** does not shut down Japan.

| Situation | Typical impact |
| --- | --- |
| **Heavy rain hour** | Minor delays, slower boarding |
| **Landslide risk areas** | Rural lines may pause (check news) |
| **Walking to station** | Covered walkways common in cities |
| **Taxis** | Easier than peak cherry chaos |

Transport habits: [getting around Japan](/guides/getting-around-japan).

Set **extra 15-20 minutes** for cross-town moves on wet days.

## Humidity and comfort expectations

Humidity makes **80°F (27°C) feel hotter** than dry summer.

| Coping | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| **Hydrate** | Vending machines everywhere |
| **Laundry** | Coin laundry if hotel slow |
| **Deodorant** | Pack your brand |
| **Hair** | Frizz is universal |
| **AC trains and shops** | Carry thin layer |
| **Mold smell** | Old buildings; normal |

You will sweat walking. That is not failure, it is June.

## Photography during rainy season

| Approach | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Embrace mist** | Temples, bamboo, neon reflections |
| **Night rain** | Shibuya crossing reflections |
| **Lens cloth** | Essential |
| **Phone in zip bag** | Touch through plastic |
| **Avoid only chasing blue sky** | Japan looks different, still good |

Hydrangea close-ups need **patience with wind**, not sunshine.

| Destination | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Tokyo** | Indoor density, covered walks |
| **Kyoto** | Gardens and hydrangeas; plan indoor blocks |
| **Osaka** | Food, arcades, shorter outdoor transfers |
| **Hokkaido** | Often less tsuyu; cooler |
| **Hakone / onsen towns** | Rain + steam aesthetic |

| Less ideal without backup | Why |

## Worst mistakes tourists make

- **Only packing fashion shoes**
- **No indoor backup** for each day
- **Ignoring hourly forecast** (rain not all-day often)
- **Skipping umbrella purchase** to save ¥500
- **Overwalking** in humidity without breaks
- **Confusing tsuyu with typhoon** and canceling too early
- **Wet trash habits** (hold litter until bin)

Trip-wide mistakes: [tourist mistakes in Japan](/guides/japan-tourist-mistakes).

## Typhoon season vs rainy season

| | **Tsuyu (rainy season)** | **Typhoon season** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **When** | Roughly May-July by region | Often **August-October** peak |
| **Rain type** | Frontal, prolonged cloudy | Intense bands, wind |
| **Travel impact** | Usually moderate | Can cancel flights, trains |
| **Planning** | Umbrella + indoor mix | Watch news, flexible tickets |

Insurance note for typhoon delays: [travel insurance guide](/guides/japan-travel-insurance).

## FAQ

### Should I cancel my June trip because of tsuyu?

Usually no. Adjust packing and daily plan. Watch forecast.

### Does it rain all day every day?

No. Many days mix dry windows with showers.

### Is Kyoto worse than Tokyo in rain?

Both get wet. Kyoto has more **outdoor** temple time; plan covered routes.

### Are hydrangeas worth it?

Late June is peak in many spots. Rain makes them vivid.

### Do I need rain boots?

Not required in cities. Water-resistant shoes enough for most tourists.

### Will trains stop?

Rare in cities for normal tsuyu. Typhoons are different.

### Is Hokkaido rain-free in June?

Not rain-free, but often **outside classic tsuyu**. Cooler and popular escape.

### Can I dry clothes in hotel room?

Slow in humidity. Coin laundry helps.

### Is June cheaper than April cherry blossom?

Often yes for lodging, depending on holidays.

### What apps for rain?

Japan Meteorological Agency info, Apple Weather, Windy for broader view.
