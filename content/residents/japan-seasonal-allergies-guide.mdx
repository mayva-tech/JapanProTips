## Cedar pollen season explained

**Sugi (杉, cedar)** and **hinoki (檜, cypress)** pollen drive much of **kafunsho (花粉症, hay fever)** talk in Japan.

| Tree | Peak feel | Region note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cedar** | Strong Feb-Apr in Kanto | Urban counts still high |
| **Cypress** | Often follows cedar | Overlaps spring |
| **Other plants** | Summer weeds, autumn ragweed pockets | Varies |

| Why it feels intense | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Large cedar forests upwind | Pollen travels into cities |
| Many people allergic | Social awareness high |
| Dry windy days | Spikes symptoms |

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents misunderstand this">
  **You can develop hay fever after moving to Japan, even with no history abroad.** If spring symptoms repeat yearly, treat it as a seasonal pattern worth discussing with a pharmacist or clinic.
</InfoBox>

## Typical allergy timelines across the year

### Seasonal allergy timeline table

| Month | Common triggers | Resident experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Jan** | Early cedar in warm years | Light symptoms start |
| **Feb** | Cedar rising | Masks appear more |
| **Mar** | Cedar peak | Peak clinic and drugstore traffic |
| **Apr** | Cedar tail, cypress | Still rough on windy days |

| **Aug-Sep** | Weeds in some areas | Regional |

Tokyo and Osaka patterns differ slightly. Use forecast apps for your city.

## FAQ

### When does cedar pollen peak in Tokyo?

Often February through April, with windy days worst. Use local forecasts.

### Can foreigners develop hay fever in Japan?

Many residents report new or stronger symptoms. A clinician can help confirm.

### Which mask is best for pollen?

Well-fitted non-woven masks commonly used in spring. Comfort matters for daily wear.

### Do air purifiers help?

Many residents find them useful for sleep. Filters must be maintained.

### Can I buy allergy medicine without a prescription?

Many OTC options exist. Ask pharmacy staff for 花粉症 products.

### Are Japanese allergy drugs strong?

Formulas differ. Read labels and ask about drowsiness.

### Should I hang laundry outside in March?

Indoor drying is common advice during peak pollen in cities.

### Do pollen apps work in English?

Major apps have English or simple charts. Yahoo and Weather News are popular.

### Is hay fever covered by insurance?

Routine OTC is usually out of pocket. Clinic visits may follow insurance rules.

### Can I wear contact lenses?

Possible, but many switch to glasses on high days.

### Is fatigue normal?

Many people report tiredness. Severe or unusual symptoms deserve professional advice.

### Does rainy season help pollen?

Rain can clear air temporarily. Mold season is a separate issue.
