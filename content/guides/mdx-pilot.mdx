## Quick answer

Most visitors need their own data in Japan. Maps, train apps, and mobile payments are easier when your phone is online on arrival.

- Install an eSIM before you fly if your phone supports it
- Pick pocket WiFi if you share one connection across several devices
- Buy a physical SIM at the airport only when you want a plastic card in hand

<RealityCheck>
  Turn on data roaming only after your Japan plan is active. If you rely on a home carrier add-on, confirm Japan coverage and daily caps first.
</RealityCheck>
