## Why grocery shopping in Japan feels different

| Difference | What you notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Smaller carts** | Apartments have tiny fridges |
| **Fresh daily mindset** | Buy often, store less |
| **Prepared food wall** | Dinner solved in one aisle |
| **Sticker discounts** | Time-based, not coupon clipping |
| **Seasonal obsession** | Strawberry week, chestnut autumn |
| **Quiet efficiency** | Fast bagging, minimal chat |

Different does not mean worse. It means **shop more often with a plan**.

## Supermarkets vs convenience stores vs drugstores

| | **Supermarket** | **Konbini** | **Drugstore (matsukiyo, etc.)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | Lowest for staples | Higher | Mid for snacks, drinks |
| **Fresh produce** | Full selection | Minimal | Limited |
| **Hours** | Often 24h or late | 24h | Until ~22:00 |
| **Best for** | Weekly stock-up | Emergency milk, eggs | Household, frozen sides |
| **Points** | Store card | App points | Drugstore points |

## Fresh food culture in Japan

| Section | Resident habit |
| --- | --- |
| **Fish** | Buy same day or next morning |
| **Vegetables** | Seasonal, washed packs common |
| **Tofu and natto** | Daily purchase |
| **Rice** | 5-10 kg bag monthly |
| **Deli** | Side dishes for busy weekdays |

Quality is high. **Sell-by dates are strict**. Respect use-by labels in small kitchens.

## Bento and prepared food sections

| Item | When to buy |
| --- | --- |
| **Bento boxes** | Lunch rush or discount hour |
| **Karaage, croquettes** | Evening sticker time |
| **Salads** | Same day only |
| **Onigiri wall** | Not supermarket only, but similar logic |

**Scenario:** Work until 8 p.m. Stop at Life supermarket at 8:20 p.m. Grab 30% off salmon bento and miso soup for ¥650 total.

## Evening discount sticker culture

| Sticker color (varies by store) | Typical discount |
| --- | --- |
| **First sticker (~30%)** | Early evening |
| **Second sticker (~50%)** | Close to closing |
| **Deep cut** | Near expiry same night |

| Tip | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Shop **30-60 min before close** | Best sticker wave |
| Learn your store's schedule | Weekends differ |
| Freeze bread and meat same night | Safe if handled fast |
| Do not open sealed packs then return | Bad etiquette |

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents get surprised by">
  Staff apply stickers **while you shop**, not only at opening. Walking the deli aisle twice can find new discounts.
</InfoBox>

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Buying **American portion sizes** of perishables
- Ignoring **freezer space** and wasting food
- Only shopping konbini (2-3x cost)
- Skipping **house brands**
- Shopping hungry at 9 p.m. without sticker plan
- Forgetting **point card** every trip
- Storing rice wrong (humidity, pests)

## FAQ

### Which supermarket is cheapest?

OK Store and Gyomu Super for staples. Neighborhood sales vary.

### Is fruit really that expensive?

Seasonal fruit can be. Citrus in winter, bananas mid-range. Gift melons are luxury tier.

### Can I use PayPay at supermarkets?

Many chains yes, after Japan bank link.

### Do I need a point card?

Optional but free points add up over a year.

### Are expiration dates strict?

Yes for fresh. Dry goods more flexible.

### Can I shop late night?

24h stores exist. Best discounts often pre-close, not after midnight.

### Where to buy cheese and bread?

KALDI, Aeon import aisle, Costco, bakery chains.

### Is frozen food low quality?

No. Japanese frozen veg and gyoza are daily staples for busy workers.

### Halal or vegetarian?

Ethnic markets and Tokyo specialty shops. Read labels for dashi in prepared food.
