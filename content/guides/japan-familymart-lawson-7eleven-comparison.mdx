## Quick answer: Which chain is best?

**No single winner.** Use the nearest good store most days. Lean **7-Eleven** for overall food and ATM reliability, **Lawson** for desserts and some hot snacks, **FamilyMart** for coffee habits and solid basics.

| If you care most about | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Overall food + ATM | **7-Eleven** |
| Desserts and sweets | **Lawson** (Uchi Cafe, etc.) |
| Coffee routine | **FamilyMart** (FamiMa Cafe) |
| Late-night hot food | Whichever is closest and stocked |
| First week in Japan | **7-Eleven** when unsure |

## Why convenience stores matter so much in Japan

Konbini are not gas-station snacks here. They are **infrastructure**.

| Role | Tourist reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Breakfast** | Before the 08:00 train |
| **Late dinner** | After everything else closed |
| **ATM** | Cash when cards fail elsewhere |
| **Bathroom** | Sometimes the only option mid-walk |
| **Wi-Fi / charging** | Emergency connectivity |
| **Weather gear** | Umbrella panic in rain |
| **Microwave meals** | Hotel room dinners |

**Example:** Rainy evening in Kyoto. Restaurants have queues. You are tired. The Lawson two minutes from the hotel becomes a sit-down meal with a ¥600 pasta cup and a pudding. That is normal Japan travel.

## 7-Eleven strengths and weaknesses

### Strengths

- Broad food quality, especially **onigiri** and hot foods
- **Seven Bank ATMs** widely used by tourists
- Strong private-label items (7Premium)
- Dense in cities and near stations
- English on many labels more often than you expect

### Weaknesses

- Peak hours can mean picked-over hot cases
- Not always the best dessert compared to Lawson
- Crowded shops near mega stations stress tired travelers

**Quick answer:** If you are overwhelmed, **7-Eleven is the safest first choice**.

## Lawson strengths and weaknesses

### Strengths

- **Desserts and bakery-style items** often shine (Uchi Cafe line)
- **Karaage chicken** has a loyal following
- Seasonal collabs feel frequent
- Calmer vibe in some residential branches

### Weaknesses

- ATM experience varies more by card
- Hot case stock can run out late in smaller shops
- Not always as dense as 7-Eleven in every micro-area

**Tourist pattern:** People who love sweets often drift Lawson.

## FamilyMart strengths and weaknesses

### Strengths

- **FamiMa Cafe** coffee for daily routine
- Solid fried chicken (**FamiChiki**)
- Good for quick breakfast + coffee combo
- Strong presence in neighborhoods and near hotels

### Weaknesses

- Food quality can feel more **store-dependent**
- Desserts good but often behind Lawson for hype
- ATM less famous than Seven Bank for foreign cards

**Tourist pattern:** Coffee-first travelers stick FamilyMart.

  - **"Post office ATMs are the same as konbini."** Different network, different hours.

## Best chain late at night

After **22:00**, quality is about **stock**, not brand.

| Late-night tip | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Station-adjacent stores | Replenished more often |
| Residential Lawson/FamilyMart | Sometimes better hot food timing |
| Avoid 23:55 panic buy | Hot case empties |

**Example:** You land late, exhausted. FamilyMart near the hotel has rice balls and coffee. That beats hunting "the best" chain across town.

## FAQ

### Which is the best convenience store in Japan?

No single best. **7-Eleven** is the safest default. **Lawson** for desserts. **FamilyMart** for coffee routines.

### Is 7-Eleven in Japan the same as in the US?

Different food system, different quality level, different private labels. Treat it as Japan-specific.

### Which konbini ATM works with foreign cards?

**Seven Bank at 7-Eleven** is the most recommended starting point. Always have a backup.

### Is Lawson or FamilyMart cheaper?

Prices are similar for basics. Sales and sizes differ slightly by product.

### Can I eat healthy at convenience stores?

Yes: salads, yogurt, eggs, nuts, lower-sugar drinks. Also easy to buy only fried food if tired.

### Which chain is best late at night?

Whichever near you is open and stocked. Station shops often refill more often.

### Are convenience stores open 24 hours?

Many urban stores are **24/7**. Some suburban stores close late night.

### Which has the best fried chicken?

Tourist debate: **Lawson karaage** vs **FamilyMart FamiChiki**. Try both once.

### Do I need to speak Japanese at konbini?

Usually no for checkout. Self-checkout scans barcodes. Hot food orders may need pointing.

### Can I pay with card at convenience stores?

Yes at most chains. Cash still useful for small towns and older machines.
