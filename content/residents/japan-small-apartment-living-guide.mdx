## Why apartment sizes in Japan surprise foreigners

| Abroad norm | Japan norm |
| --- | --- |
| **Bedroom separate from kitchen** | One room plus kitchen niche |
| **Large closets** | Narrow wandrobe, loft storage |
| **In-unit laundry** | Often balcony or coin laundry |
| **Central heating** | Wall AC per room |
| **Dishwasher standard** | Rare in budget units |
| **Thick walls** | Sound travels |

Small is not temporary for most residents. It is **the default urban contract**.

## Typical apartment layouts explained

Japanese listings use **floor plan codes**, not bedroom counts alone.

| Zone | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **R (room)** | Main living and sleeping space combined |
| **K (kitchen)** | Separate kitchen nook, not full room |
| **DK (dining-kitchen)** | Kitchen plus space for small table |
| **LDK** | Living, dining, kitchen in one open area |
| **S (service room)** | Small extra closet-sized room |
| **Loft** | Raised sleeping platform |

| Layout sketch (concept) | Feel |
| --- | --- |
| **1R** | Studio, kitchen along wall |
| **1K** | Slight separation, kitchen behind door |
| **1DK** | Table possible, less bed-kitchen overlap |
| **1LDK** | Rare upgrade, family viable |

Measure **door width, hallway turn, and balcony depth** before buying furniture.

## 1R vs 1K vs 1DK vs 1LDK

| Type | Approx size (urban) | Best for | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1R** | 18-25 m² | Single, short stay | No kitchen door, smell and sound |
| **1K** | 20-30 m² | Single worker | Kitchen still tiny |
| **1DK** | 25-35 m² | Couple, WFH desk | Higher rent |
| **1LDK** | 35-45 m²+ | Couple, pet sometimes | Harder in central wards |

| Comparison | 1R | 1K | 1DK | 1LDK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kitchen separation | No | Partial | Yes | Yes |
| Desk space | Tight | Possible | Easier | Easier |
| Couple comfort | Low | Low-mid | Mid | High |
| Rent (same area) | Lowest | Low | Mid | Highest |
| Cooking smell in bed area | High | Medium | Lower | Lower |

## Storage realities and organization culture

Japan sells **vertical storage** because floor space is scarce.

| Storage zone | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Closet (押入れ / クローゼット)** | Seasonal clothes, bedding |
| **Above kitchen cabinets** | Bins, rarely used items |
| **Under bed** | Flat boxes, off-season shoes |
| **Balcony** | Shoes, drying, illegal for clutter in some buildings |
| **Genkan** | Shoes only, not extra warehouse |

| Realistic storage example (1K) | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Closet one | Winter coats, vacuum bag clothes |
| Closet two | Sheets, towels |
| Three IKEA boxes under bed | Summer clothes, cables |
| Wall shelf | Books, spices |
| Over-door hook | Bags, jacket |

| Product culture | Why it exists |
| --- | --- |
| **Compression bags** | Reduce volume |
| **Clear drawers** | See contents |
| **Tension rods** | Instant shelf in closet |
| **100 yen boxes** | Uniform sizing |

## Bathroom and unit bath realities

**Unit bath (UB, ユニットバス)** combines tub, shower, toilet zone in one molded room. For a full soak, many residents use neighborhood [sento or super sento](/residents/japan-public-bath-sauna-guide).

| Feature | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Plastic walls** | Easy clean, echo sound |
| **Deep tub** | Soak culture, not only shower |
| **Toilet in same room or separate** | Layout varies |
| **Fan button** | Run during and after shower |
| **Small sink** | Minimal counter |

| Habit | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Dry floor after shower | Mold prevention |
| Store products in wall rack | Surface space |
| Wash laundry in tub sometimes | Older habit, less common now |

## FAQ

### Is 20 m² too small to live long term?

Many singles live years in 1R or 1K. Comfort depends on habits and storage discipline.

### What is the best layout for a couple?

Usually 1DK or 1LDK. 1K works only with minimal stuff and aligned schedules.

### How do I reduce cooking smells in a 1R?

Use hood, lid on pots, avoid deep frying, air out after.

### Are Japanese apartments noisy?

Older wooden and light steel buildings transmit sound. Concrete towers are better but not silent.

### Can I work from home in a 1K?

Yes with a wall desk, headphones, and boundary rules.

### Where do I put winter clothes?

Vacuum bags under bed or top closet shelf, seasonal swap.

### Is a washing machine always included?

Often in newer units. Confirm listing. Coin laundry is backup.

### Can I install extra shelves?

Usually yes with non-damage tension rods. Drilling needs landlord OK.

### How do I prevent mold?

Fan after shower, dehumidify, do not overpack closet.

### Are futons better than beds?

Futons save space. Beds are easier for daily comfort. Personal choice.

### Can I have a desk and a bed in 1R?

Yes with loft bed or fold-down desk.

### Do small apartments cost less to heat?

One room can be cheaper if you heat one zone. Poor insulation still hurts.
