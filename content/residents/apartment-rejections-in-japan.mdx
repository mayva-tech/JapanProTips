## Quick answer: Why do foreigners get rejected?

**Landlords and guarantor companies assess risk with paperwork you may not have yet:** stable income, long visa, Japanese emergency contact, and prior rental history in Japan.

| Factor | Screening weight |
| --- | --- |
| Visa length remaining | High |
| Income vs rent ratio | High |
| Guarantor company approval | Gatekeeper |
| Foreigner-friendly listing | Pre-filter |
| Pets, smoking, occupation | Variable |

**Quick answer:** Apply only to listings marked foreigner OK or with English-speaking agencies. Improve one weak point (income proof, guarantor, visa) instead of mass applying.

## Emotional reality

Rejection feels personal. Often it is **template risk scoring**, not a comment on you. Many residents apply to 5-15 units before approval.

| Feeling | Reframe |
| --- | --- |
| "They hate foreigners" | Some buildings ban, others welcome |
| "My Japanese is the problem" | Income and visa matter more |
| "I will never find a place" | Timeline issue, not forever |

## FAQ

### Can I appeal a rejection?

Rarely. Move to next listing with stronger file.

### Do all buildings reject foreigners?

No. Many Tokyo wards have foreigner-friendly stock.

### Does speaking Japanese help?

Helps with agents. Guarantor math matters more.

### Student visa impossible?

Harder, not impossible. Share houses and student dorms exist.

### Rejection on credit score?

Japan uses different proof (income, guarantor), not US FICO.
