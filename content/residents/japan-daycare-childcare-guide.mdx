You are not behind. **Local parents also stress waitlists**.

## Hoikuen vs yochien explained

| | **Hoikuen (保育園)** | **Yochien (幼稚園)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main role** | Childcare while parents work | Preschool education |
| **Typical hours** | 7:00-18:00 or longer | 9:00-14:00 often |
| **Age** | 0-5 (until elementary) | Often 3-5 |
| **Meals** | Lunch and snacks included | Lunch often |
| **Holidays** | Fewer, care focused | School calendar |
| **Best when** | Both parents work full time | One parent flexible, education focus |

| **Kodomoen (こども園)** | Hybrid facility, ward labels vary |
| --- | --- |
| **Ninka (認可)** | Authorized, subsidized |
| **Mu ninka (無認可)** | Private hours, different fees |

After hoikuen years, children enter **elementary school** (see [school system guide](/residents/japan-school-system)).

<InfoBox title="Foreign parents misunderstand this">
  **"Yochien is free daycare."** Yochien is usually **shorter hours** and not a full workplace solution. Working parents often need **hoikuen** or extended care options.
</InfoBox>

## Waiting list realities

Urban areas (Tokyo wards, Yokohama, Osaka) often have **more applicants than seats** for popular hoikuen.

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Central ward address** | Higher competition |
| **Baby boom year** | Harder cohort |
| **Popular facility** | Longer wait |
| **Sibling already inside** | Big advantage |
| **Both parents working** | Points up in many wards |

| Realistic outcome | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| **Accepted first choice** | Lucky, confirm early |
| **Offered distant site** | Commute tradeoff |
| **Wait until space opens** | Backup care needed |
| **Start at 1-2 years** | Not always age 0 |

**Scenario:** Couple in Setagaya, both full-time jobs. Apply at birth for hoikuen start at 6 months. Accepted at 10 months at second-choice site 20 min walk away.

## Application procedures and timing

Applications usually go through **your ward or city childcare portal** (names vary: 保育所申請, 子育て支援, etc.).

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Register residence at ward office |
| 2 | Get My Number and childcare paperwork ready |
| 3 | Attend orientation (often required) |
| 4 | List preferred facilities in order |
| 5 | Submit application by deadline |
| 6 | Receive lottery or allocation result |
| 7 | Confirm acceptance and pay fees |

| Timing example | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Child born January** | Register birth, ask ward desk |
| **March** | Attend briefing for April cycle |
| **April application window** | Submit ranked list |
| **May result** | Start July or October |
| **April start (next year)** | Common for yochien |

Ward desk help: [city hall guide](/residents/japan-city-hall-guide). Address matters: [neighborhood guide](/residents/japan-neighborhood-guide).

## FAQ

### Can foreign children attend public hoikuen?

Yes. Residence registration and application rules apply same as Japanese families.

### When should I apply?

As early as your ward allows, often soon after birth for urban hoikuen.

### Is hoikuen mandatory?

No. Compulsory education starts at elementary school age.

### What is the difference between hoikuen and yochien?

Hoikuen is long-hour childcare. Yochien is shorter preschool-style education.

### How much does daycare cost in Tokyo?

Varies by age and income. Babies cost more. Many 3-5 authorized seats have low tuition plus meal fees.

### Do both parents need to work for hoikuen priority?

Many wards give points for dual income. Rules vary. Check local PDF.

### What if we are not accepted?

Waitlist, try other wards facilities, unauthorized care, babysitter, or adjust work hours temporarily.

### Are international preschools worth it?

If short stay or English continuity matters, yes. If long-term Japan life, local hoikuen builds Japanese peer network.

### What fever keeps a child home?

Often 37.5C or higher. Facility posts exact chart at entrance.

### Can grandparents pick up?

Register authorized pickers on forms.

### Is lunch provided?

Usually yes at hoikuen. Allergy forms required.

### Does NHI cover child doctor visits?

Yes, with copay. See health insurance guide.

### Can we apply before apartment lease in ward?

Usually need registered address in that municipality.
