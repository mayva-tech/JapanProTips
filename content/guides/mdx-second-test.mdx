## Quick answer

You do not need both Suica and PASMO. Pick whichever card is easier to buy at your first station machine.

- Same tap-to-pay network on trains, buses, and many shops
- Both work on airport rail lines into Tokyo
- Top up with cash at station machines

<TipBox>
  If your phone supports Mobile Suica or PASMO, you can skip a plastic card for short trips.
</TipBox>

## Suica vs PASMO at a glance

| Topic | Suica | PASMO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Branding | JR East | Private metro operators |
| Where to buy | JR stations, airport JR desks | Metro stations, airport metro desks |
| Day-to-day use | Same IC network | Same IC network |
| Best for | Arriving on JR lines | Arriving on Keikyu or metro-first |
