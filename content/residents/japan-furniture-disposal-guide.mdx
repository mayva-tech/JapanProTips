## Why trash disposal in Japan shocks foreign residents

| Expectation abroad | Japan reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Curbside bulk pickup** | Reservation and stickers |
| **One trash day for all** | Categories and sizes split |
| **Free fridge drop-off** | Recycling fee for appliances |
| **Donate anytime** | Shops reject stained items |
| **Landlord handles everything** | Tenant pays for left items |

The system is **organized, not impossible**. Once you know your ward rules, disposal becomes a checklist.

## What counts as oversized trash (sodai gomi)

**Sodai gomi (粗大ごみ)** means items too large for regular burnable or non-burnable bags.

| Usually sodai gomi | Usually regular trash |
| --- | --- |
| Sofa, bed frame, mattress | Small broken items in bags |
| Table, bookshelf, desk | Cardboard broken down flat |
| Bicycle (some wards) | Kitchen trash |
| Large rug | Small electronics in e-waste |
| Suitcase, large toys | Clothes in textile bags |

| Size rule (typical) | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Longest side over 30 cm | Often sodai category |
| Under limit per ward chart | May be non-burnable instead |
| Weight over one person | Plan two-person carry |

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents misunderstand this">
  **Sodai gomi is not "free big trash day."** You buy a sticker or pay a fee, reserve pickup or drop-off, and follow size categories. Putting items out without stickers is not accepted.
</InfoBox>

### Oversized trash examples

| Item | Typical category | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office chair | Sodai | Remove metal if ward asks split |

| Mattress | Sodai | Plastic wrap may be required |

## Furniture disposal process step-by-step

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Check your **ward/city sodai gomi page** (Japanese OK with translate) |
| 2 | Measure longest side and weight |
| 3 | Buy correct **sticker(s)** at convenience store or ward office |
| 4 | Attach sticker where instructed (visible) |
| 5 | **Reserve pickup** by phone, app, or web if required |
| 6 | Place at collection point **only on assigned morning** |
| 7 | Keep receipt or confirmation until collected |

| Drop-off vs pickup | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Curbside pickup** | Most apartments use station or street point |
| **Ward center drop-off** | Faster if you have a car |
| **Private pickup company** | Tight move-out deadline |

## Sodai gomi stickers explained

Stickers are **prepaid disposal fees**. Price bands depend on size class.

| Size class (example labels) | Illustrative fee (Tokyo-style) |
| --- | --- |
| S (chair, small table) | ¥200-¥800 |
| M (desk, single mattress) | ¥800-¥2,000 |
| L (sofa, large shelf) | ¥2,000-¥5,000 |
| XL (bed frame, large sofa) | ¥5,000+ |

| Where to buy | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Convenience store | Limited stock |
| Ward office | Full chart |
| Online (some cities) | QR sticker print |

| Sticker rules | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| One item one sticker | Usually |
| Visible placement | Front face of item |
| Wrong sticker size | Not collected, fine risk |
| Multiple parts | May need multiple stickers |

Fees differ by municipality. Always use **your** city's chart, not a friend's ward.

## Appliance recycling laws

Japan's **Home Appliance Recycling Law** covers designated items. They need **licensed recycling**, not sodai stickers alone.

| Designated appliances | Recycling route |
| --- | --- |
| **Air conditioner** | Retailer take-back or licensed center |
| **TV** | Same |
| **Refrigerator / freezer** | Same |
| **Washer / dryer** | Same |

| Who pays | Typical |
| --- | --- |
| When buying new | Retailer collects old on delivery |
| Disposal only | You pay recycling fee + transport |

## FAQ

### Can I put furniture out without a sticker?

No in almost all municipalities. Items will not be collected.

### Where do I buy sodai gomi stickers?

Convenience stores, ward offices, sometimes online portals.

### How do I dispose of a mattress?

Usually sodai gomi with correct size sticker. Some wards require plastic wrap.

### Can I throw a microwave in sodai gomi?

Depends on ward size rules. Check chart.

### How much does it cost to dispose of a sofa?

Often ¥2,000-¥5,000 sticker class in major cities. Confirm locally.

### Do I need to disassemble furniture?

If over size limit, yes. IKEa-style tables often need breakdown.

### What about batteries and small electronics?

Separate small appliance or e-waste collection, not sodai.

### Can my moving company throw everything away?

Some offer disposal add-ons. Verify licensed appliance handling.

### Will illegal dumping affect my visa?

Not directly. Landlord and city fines are the usual risk.

### What if stickers are sold out?

Try another store, ward office, or reschedule pickup.

### Can I leave items for the next tenant?

Only with landlord written OK. Otherwise you pay disposal.

### Is burning old wood furniture allowed?

No. Use sodai or licensed disposal.
