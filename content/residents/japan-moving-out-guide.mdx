## Why moving out in Japan needs planning

Japanese move-outs reward calendars, not last-minute packing. Cleaning standards, garbage rules, and utility final readings all have fixed dates tied to your keys.

- Notice periods are often one to two months on standard leases.
- Restoration fees may be fixed in the contract regardless of how clean you leave the unit.
- Utilities bill through the move date; internet contracts may need separate cancellation.
- City hall expects address updates within the required window.

<InfoBox title="Watch for this">
  Read your contract renewal and termination clauses before you tell your employer or school. Some leases penalize early exit or require finding a replacement tenant.
</InfoBox>

## Check your rental contract first

Your **lease** (賃貸借契約) and **move-out rules** sheet list notice length, cleaning fees, and who schedules inspection.

- Highlight the termination article and any **restoration** (原状回復) notes.
- Check whether you must use a designated cleaning company.
- Note if the management company mediates all contact with the owner.
- Save PDFs and LINE threads in one folder labeled with dates.

## Notice periods and move-out date

Most contracts require **written notice** one or two months before the last day you occupy the room.

- Send notice by the method the contract names: email, fax, or paper form.
- Align your last night, key return, and inspection on the contract timeline.
- Prorated rent usually ends on the agreed date, not the day you finish packing.
- Weekend move-outs are fine if keys return Monday when the office opens.

<TipBox title="Field note">
  Photograph your notice email or stamped form. Disputes later often hinge on the date management received it.
</TipBox>

## Contacting your real estate company or landlord

**Management** (管理会社) often handles day-to-day move-out steps even when a landlord owns the building.

- Ask for the move-out packet: inspection form, garbage rules, and cleaning vendor list.
- Confirm inspection time, key return location, and bank account for deposit return.
- Report damage you plan to repair before inspection if the contract allows.
- Use polite, dated Japanese or ask a friend to call if written English is not accepted.

## Cleaning fees and restoration costs

Many leases include a **cleaning fee** (クリーニング費) or **restoration fee** charged at move-out.

| Fee type | What it usually covers |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed cleaning fee | Professional clean regardless of condition |
| Restoration | Wall holes, flooring beyond normal wear |
| Optional self-clean | Rare; only if contract explicitly allows |

- Normal wear from daily life differs from cigarette burns or unfilled nail holes.
- Professional cleaners follow company checklists, not your personal standard.
- Paying the listed fee does not always waive all damage charges after inspection.

## Damage checks and inspection day

**Inspection** (退去立会い) walks the unit with management and notes scratches, stains, and equipment.

- Arrive with the room empty, utilities still on for lights if needed.
- Compare to your move-in condition report if you received one.
- Take photos of each room after cleaning, before they arrive.
- Sign the sheet only after you understand marked items.

<InfoBox>
  Disagree on the spot if something was pre-existing. Adding notes on the paper beats arguing by email weeks later without photos.
</InfoBox>

## Utilities cancellation

Cancel or transfer each utility by your last occupancy day. Final bills may arrive after you leave.

### Electricity

Call or use the web portal for your **electric** provider. Give meter reading date and forwarding address.

- Shut off breakers after final reading if instructed.
- Keep account number from recent bills.

### Gas

**Gas** companies require an appointment to close the meter in many buildings.

- Someone adult usually must be present for the shutoff visit.
- Confirm pilot lights are off after closure.

### Water

**Water** is billed through the city or a regional bureau tied to your address.

- Notify on the ward water desk or via the form management provides.
- Final sewage and water may bill separately.

### Internet

**Internet** contracts often need weeks of notice. See the internet setup guide for transfer vs cancel.

- Return rented routers with tracking numbers.
- Early termination fees apply on promo plans.

## Oversized garbage disposal

Furniture and appliances use **oversized garbage** (粗大ごみ), not regular burnable bags.

- Book pickup on the ward website or phone line.
- Buy the sticker or pay the fee your city requires.
- Do not leave sofas in the garbage room without booking.
- See the garbage rules guide for category basics.

## Address change procedures

Update your **registered address** at city hall when your new home is fixed.

- Moving-out registration at the old city and moving-in at the new one.
- Residence card address updates follow immigration rules.
- Health insurance and pension mail route to the registered record.
- Use the residence registration guide for timing.

## Mail forwarding

**Japan Post forwarding** (転送) sends mail from the old address for a set period.

- Apply online or at the post office before you move.
- Forwarding does not replace updating bank and phone addresses.
- Set end date and new address in Japanese format.

## Returning keys

Return **all keys**, mailbox keys, and parking remotes on the contract date.

- Missing keys trigger rekey fees on many leases.
- Get a receipt or signed key return sheet when possible.
- Mailbox emptying prevents leftover letters from blocking deposit talks.

## Getting your deposit back

**Deposit** (敷金) and **key money** (礼金) are not the same. Deposit may be refunded after deductions.

- Refund timing is often one to two months after inspection, sometimes longer.
- Deductions list cleaning, damage, and unpaid rent or utilities.
- Bank account details must match the name on the lease.
- No guide can promise a full refund; disputes go through management channels.

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Giving verbal notice without written record
- Canceling internet on move day instead of weeks earlier
- Leaving oversized items in the building garbage room
- Forgetting gas shutoff appointment
- Skipping city hall address change while mail still arrives at the old ward

## Simple move-out checklist

- Read termination and cleaning clauses in the lease
- Send written notice by the required date
- Book gas shutoff, electric final reading, and internet cancel or transfer
- Schedule oversized garbage for furniture
- Clean or hire the vendor management names
- Walk inspection with photos and move-in sheet in hand
- Return all keys and get return proof
- File postal forwarding and city hall address change
- Confirm deposit refund bank details in writing

## Final advice

Move-out is project management with a mop. Start from the contract date and work backward six weeks. Most stress comes from utilities and internet lead times, not from boxing dishes.

Keep one email thread with management and label every attachment. Calm paper trails beat confident memory when a cleaning charge appears later.
