## Why sending money from Japan confuses foreigners

| Confusion | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Bank advertised "international transfer" | Often expensive SWIFT with opaque FX |
| App shows low fee | **Exchange margin** may be the real cost |
| US apps (Venmo, Zelle) | Do not replace Japan to abroad corridors |
| Same-day everywhere | Corridors vary by country |
| Cash only option | Online often cheaper |

You need a **Japanese bank account** or approved provider KYC for most paths.

## Bank transfers vs remittance services

| | **Japanese bank international transfer** | **Online remittance (Wise, etc.)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Setup** | Branch or online banking | App signup + verification |
| **Fees** | Fixed + receiving bank fees | Upfront fee shown |
| **FX rate** | Bank rate, often weak | Mid-market or close on good apps |
| **Speed** | 2-5 business days common | Hours to 2 days many corridors |
| **Best for** | Large formal transfers, some employers | Regular family sends, savings home |
| **English** | Variable at branch | App often English |

| | **Cash remittance (Western Union style)** | **Online to bank account** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pickup** | Recipient cash at agent | Recipient bank deposit |
| **Fee** | Higher often | Lower often |
| **Speed** | Minutes to hours possible | Depends on banking rails |
| **Risk** | Cash handling | Account errors delay |

## Common services residents use

### Wise

- Popular for **mid-market style** pricing transparency
- Fund from Japanese bank via transfer
- Strong for US, UK, EU, Australia, many Asian banks
- ID verification online

### Western Union / MoneyGram

- **Cash pickup** worldwide network
- Konbini or agent locations in Japan
- Useful when recipient has no bank account

### Bank transfers (SWIFT)

- Your **MUFG, SMBC, Japan Post Bank**, etc.
- SWIFT code and recipient IBAN/account details required
- Higher total cost on small amounts common

### Online remittance apps

Regional apps serve specific corridors (Philippines, Vietnam, Nepal, etc.). Residents in communities often recommend corridor-specific brands. Compare fee + rate on the exact amount you send.

| Service type | Fee mindset | FX | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wise (example) | Low stated fee | Competitive | 1-2 days |
| Major bank SWIFT | ¥2,000-¥7,500+ send fee | Bank margin | 2-5 days |
| Cash remittance | Medium-high | Retail rate | Fast pickup |
| Corridor specialist app | Varies | Varies | Same day possible |

Numbers are illustrations. Run a quote for your amount.

## Fees and exchange rate realities

Total cost = **transfer fee** + **exchange rate margin** + **recipient bank fee**.

**Example sending ¥100,000 to USD:**

| Method | Illustrative outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Strong online remittance | Recipient gets more dollars, fee ¥500-¥1,500 |
| Traditional bank | Recipient gets fewer dollars, fee ¥3,000+ |

Always compare **amount recipient receives**, not only sender fee.

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents get confused by this">
  "Zero fee" marketing can hide a **bad exchange rate**. Two providers with ¥0 fee are not equal.
</InfoBox>

## Identity verification requirements

Providers require **KYC** (know your customer):

| Document | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Residence card | ID and visa status |
| Passport | Backup ID |
| My Number notification or card | Sometimes requested |
| Japanese address | Utility bill or residence certificate |
| Selfie | Liveness check on apps |

Verification can take **minutes to 2 business days** on first setup.

My Number context: [My Number Card guide](/residents/japan-my-number-card).

## My Number and residency requirements

- Legitimate remittance companies comply with **anti-money laundering** laws
- They may ask purpose of transfer for large amounts
- **Residence card** expiry date matters; update profile when renewed
- Transfers tied to **your name** only; do not send on behalf of undisclosed third parties

## Limits and compliance checks

| Limit type | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Per transfer cap** | Provider and corridor specific |
| **Daily / monthly cap** | Increases after history |
| **Bank online limit** | Separate from app limit |
| **Large transfers** | Source of funds questions |

**Example:** First Wise transfer ¥50,000 succeeds. Later ¥1,000,000 transfer triggers extra review. Normal compliance, not personal accusation.

## Cash transfers vs online transfers

| Cash pickup | Online to bank |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient without bank account | Recipient has account |
| Higher fee often | Better rates often |
| Fast in emergencies | Better audit trail |
| Risk of cash loss on pickup | Wrong account number painful |

Use cash networks when necessary, not by default.

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Wrong **SWIFT/BIC** or account number (one digit fails)
- Sending **USD** when recipient account is local currency only
- Ignoring **recipient bank fee** deduction
- Multiple small transfers instead of one (fees multiply)
- Not updating **expired residence card** on app profile
- Falling for **social media "best rate"** unlicensed brokers
- Forgetting **transfer purpose** field on tax-sensitive sums

## Best practices for minimizing fees

| Practice | Save |
| --- | --- |
| Compare **recipient receives** amount | Real benchmark |
| Send **larger amounts less often** | Fewer fixed fees |
| Use **online remittance** for recurring sends | Better FX |
| Avoid **double conversion** (JPY to USD to local) if direct corridor exists | Rate stack |
| Lock rate when app offers **guaranteed rate window** | Volatility |
| Keep **recipient details saved** | Fewer typos |

Credit card funding sometimes works but may count as cash advance. Read card terms: [credit cards in Japan](/residents/japan-credit-cards).

## FAQ

### Can I send money without a Japanese bank account?

Difficult. Most licensed paths require Japan-based funding. Cash agents may accept cash in person with ID.

### Is Wise legal in Japan?

Wise operates with appropriate licensing for many corridors. Confirm current status on their site.

### How much does a bank charge?

Varies by bank and amount. Ask for **total cost including exchange** before confirming.

### Can I use PayPal to send home?

Possible for some countries as personal payments. Fees and FX differ from remittance specialists.

### How do I send to someone without a bank account?

Western Union, MoneyGram, or similar cash pickup networks.

### Will my employer see transfers?

Employer does not see personal bank sends. Your bank statement shows outbound payments.

### What is SWIFT?

International bank messaging network. Slow but universal.

### Can I send in USD from Japan?

Some apps let you hold USD balance. Often JPY out of Japanese salary is simpler.

### How long is Wise verification?

Minutes to 48 hours typical.

### Do I need My Number to send money?

Not always. Providers list required documents. May ask for tax ID in some countries on recipient side.
