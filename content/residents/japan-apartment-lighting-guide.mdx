## Why apartment lighting feels different in Japan

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Single main ceiling unit** | One switch, whole room |
| **High color temperature default** | Cool white common |
| **Small windows in urban units** | Less natural fill |
| **Paper doors (shoji) rare in cheap units** | Hard flat light |
| **No built-in bedside lamps** | You bring your own |
| **Ceiling outlet (天井コンセント)** | For owner light only |

Lighting shapes **mood and sleep** more than furniture in year one.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Expecting warm hotel-style lighting on move-in. **Rental ceiling units** are often bright and neutral.

## FAQ

### Do apartments come with ceiling lights?

Often yes in urban rentals. **Confirm viewing photos**. Some are empty hook only.

### Can I change the bulb color?

If you own the unit or lease allows. **Swapping whole fixture** needs permission.

### What voltage are bulbs in Japan?

**100V**. Buy Japan-market bulbs.

### Warm or cool for small apartment?

Many residents use **cool or neutral ceiling plus warm lamp** at night.

### How do I reduce glare on bed?

Angle lamp away, use **diffuser**, dim ceiling.

### Do LED remotes need batteries?

Yes. **Keep spare** or use wall switch only.

### Will better lighting lower electricity bill?

Slightly with LED. **AC dominates** summer bill.
