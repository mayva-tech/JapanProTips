## Why visa renewal causes stress for foreign residents

| Stress | Why it feels heavy |
| --- | --- |
| **Japanese forms** | Official names must match exactly |
| **Employer dependence** | Company documents for many statuses |
| **Tax and pension** | Past compliance questioned |
| **Waiting period** | Life plans pause on a stamp |
| **Fear of denial** | Rare for stable cases, but real |
| **Crowded offices** | Appointments and long lines |

Most renewals for employed residents with clean records are **routine**. Stress often comes from starting late, not from random rejection.

## Residence status vs visa explained

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Status of residence (在留資格)** | Your legal category: Engineer, Student, Spouse, etc. |
| **Period of stay (在留期間)** | End date on residence card |
| **Residence card (在留カード)** | Wallet ID with chip |
| **Visa sticker in passport** | Related but card is daily proof |
| **Renewal / extension** | Apply before period ends to continue same status |
| **Change of status** | Switch category (different application) |

Colloquially people say "visa renewal." Officials say **extension of period of stay** for the same status.

## Typical renewal timelines

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **3 months before expiry** | Read ISA guidance for your status |
| **2 months before** | Request employer documents |
| **1-2 months before** | File application (common window) |
| **Last 2 weeks** | Risky if documents missing |
| **After expiry without approval** | Overstay (avoid) |

**Timeline example (Engineer, card expires 31 October):**

| Date | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 1 August | Employer issues employment certificate |
| 15 August | Book online immigration slot |
| 1 September | Submit application in person or by mail if eligible |
| September-October | Processing, possible document request |
| Mid-October | Receive new card or extension stamp |
| 31 October | Old period ends (must have receipt or new card) |

Processing often **1-3 months**. Apply early.

## Immigration office realities

| Topic | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Location** | Regional immigration bureau for your address |
| **Language** | Japanese primary; some English pamphlets |
| **Hours** | Weekday daytime, crowded lunch |
| **Security** | ID check at entrance |
| **Waiting** | Number tickets even with reservation |
| **Questions** | Short, factual: job, salary, address |

Bring **printed copies** and originals. Dress neat, not formal suit required.

## Common documents required

Packages vary by status. Typical **employed worker** set:

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Application form** | Official ISA form for extension |
| **Passport** | Valid, all pages requested sometimes |
| **Residence card** | Front and back copy |
| **Photo** | Spec size per form |
| **Employment certificate (在職証明書)** | Company letterhead |
| **Company registry or financial docs** | Employer stability |
| **Tax withholding slip (源泉徴収票)** | Prior year |
| **Resident tax certificate (住民税)** | From ward office |
| **Reason statement** | Why you continue activity in Japan |

**Document checklist by applicant type:**

| Type | Extra documents |
| --- | --- |
| **Company employee** | Contract copy, salary proof |
| **Student** | Transcript, attendance, sponsor |
| **Spouse** | Marriage cert, spouse income, interview |
| **Self-employed** | Tax returns, contracts, bank flow |

## Employment and income verification

Immigration checks whether your **activity matches your status**.

| Status | They look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Engineer / Specialist** | Real job related to field |
| **Instructor** | Teaching hours and employer |
| **Student** | School progress, part-time permit |
| **Dependent** | Sponsor support |

**Scenario:** You changed jobs 2 months ago. Bring **new employment contract**, hire date letter, and explanation letter. Gap without work raises questions.

Job search context: [finding jobs in Japan](/residents/japan-job-hunting-guide) if you are switching legally.

## Tax and pension payment importance

Unpaid **resident tax**, **national health insurance**, or **pension** can weaken an application.

| Record | Where to get proof |
| --- | --- |
| **Resident tax** | Ward office certificate of payment |
| **Income tax** | 源泉徴収票 or tax office documents |
| **Pension** | Nenkin net statement |

Guides: [Japanese taxes](/residents/japan-taxes-guide), [pension system](/residents/japan-pension-system).

Pay arrears **before** applying when possible.

## My Number and residence card relationship

| Card | Role in renewal |
| --- | --- |
| **Residence card** | Proves legal stay; must match address |
| **My Number notification** | Sometimes requested for tax link |
| **My Number Card (optional)** | Not always required for immigration |

Update **address on residence card** within 14 days of moving. Mismatch causes delays.

My Number guide: [My Number Card for foreign residents](/residents/japan-my-number-card).

Ward office basics: [city hall guide](/residents/japan-city-hall-guide).

## Online reservation and waiting systems

| System | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Online reservation (Web予約)** | Pick date and time slot |
| **My Number login** | Sometimes used for gov portals |
| **Walk-in** | Possible but long wait |

Book **early morning** slots. Arrive 15 minutes before with full document set.

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents panic about this">
  **No appointment does not mean no line.** Walk-in can take half a day in Tokyo busy seasons (March, July, September).
</InfoBox>

## Changing jobs before renewal

| Situation | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Same status, new employer** | Notify immigration within 14 days of new job (change notification rules apply) |
| **Gap between jobs** | Short gap with explanation; long gap risky |
| **Different job category** | May need change of status, not simple renewal |
| **Unemployment** | Seek immigration lawyer early |

Do not hide job changes. Bring **both old and new** company papers if transition recent.

## Freelancers and self-employed residents

| Requirement | Typical proof |
| --- | --- |
| **Business legitimacy** | Contracts, invoices |
| **Income** | Tax return (確定申告) copies |
| **Activity match** | Portfolio for design, engineering, etc. |
| **Insurance and tax** | Paid resident tax certificates |

Self-employed renewals get **more scrutiny**. Organize 12 months of records.

Bank statements help: [open a bank account](/residents/open-bank-account-japan).

## Student visa renewal basics

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Attendance** | Low attendance is major risk |
| **Progress** | Credits or language level |
| **Part-time work** | Stay within permitted hours |
| **Sponsor funds** | Bank balance proof |
| **School letter** | Required from institution |

Plan renewal with **international student office** 2 months ahead.

## Spouse visa considerations

| Topic | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Marriage continuity** | Still living together |
| **Household** | Shared lease or utilities |
| **Income** | Japanese spouse or combined support |
| **Interview** | Possible questions on daily life |
| **Divorce pending** | Get professional advice immediately |

Spouse renewals focus on **genuine marriage and stability**, not only love story letters.

## What happens during processing

| Stage | Your experience |
| --- | --- |
| **Receipt issued** | 申請受理 sticker or postcard |
| **Processing** | Wait weeks; stay in Japan |
| **Additional documents** | Respond quickly if requested |
| **Approval** | New card mail or pick-up notice |
| **Denial** | Explanation letter, appeal options limited |

While pending with valid receipt, you generally **may work** if prior permission continued (confirm your receipt wording).

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Applying **too late**
- **Expired passport** discovered at counter
- Address on forms does not match **residence card**
- Missing **resident tax payment** certificate
- Employer letter missing **stamp or hire date**
- Assuming **English documents** alone are enough
- Working **outside permitted activity** before renewal
- Not reporting **marriage or divorce**

## What immigration officers actually care about

| Priority | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Legal activity** | You do what your status allows |
| **Stable support** | Salary or sponsor covers life |
| **Compliance** | Taxes, insurance, notifications |
| **Consistency** | Story matches documents |
| **No fraud** | Real marriage, real job |

They are not testing your Japanese trivia. They verify **paper truth**.

## FAQ

### When should I apply for renewal?

Often 1-3 months before expiry. Earlier is safer if documents need time.

### Can I travel while renewal is processing?

Risky without consultation. Short domestic travel usually fine. International travel may be denied re-entry without valid status proof.

### What if my renewal is denied?

You may need to change status, leave Japan, or appeal depending on case. Lawyer help recommended.

### Do I need a Japanese guarantor for renewal?

Not usually for employed adults. Student and some statuses differ.

### Is My Number Card mandatory for renewal?

Not always. Carry residence card and passport.

### Can I renew online completely?

Some steps digital; many statuses still require in-person or mail per current rules. Check ISA site.

### What if my passport expires soon?

Renew passport at embassy first. Immigration needs valid passport.

### Does part-time work affect renewal?

Legal part-time within permit is fine. Illegal work hurts badly.

### How long is the new period?

1 year, 3 years, or 5 years depending on status and history.

### Should I hire an immigration lawyer?

Optional for simple renewals. Helpful for gaps, criminal records, divorce, or company closure.
