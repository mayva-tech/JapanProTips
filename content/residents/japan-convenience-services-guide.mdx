## Why convenience stores become part of daily life in Japan

| Factor | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Density** | Often one within a few minutes on foot |
| **Hours** | Many open 24 hours |
| **Payment rails** | Utilities and tax use konbini barcodes |
| **ATMs** | Cash when banks are closed |
| **Services** | Print, tickets, pickup in one stop |

Residents who ignore konbini services often overpay for time, miss bill deadlines, or drive to a bank branch for tasks a 3-minute walk could solve.

## Bill payment systems explained

**Konbini payment (コンビニ決済)** uses a barcode or payment number on a paper slip or email. Staff scan it, you pay cash, they stamp the slip.

| Bill type | Konbini common? | Typical deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Electricity** | Yes | ~10 days after meter reading |
| **Gas** | Yes | Similar |
| **Water** | Yes | Ward varies |
| **Internet / mobile** | Often | Invoice date + grace |
| **Resident tax** | Yes | Installment schedule |
| **Insurance** | Sometimes | Policy letter |
| **Amazon / online orders** | Yes (prepay) | Order-specific |

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Bring slip or show barcode on phone |
| 2 | Hand to cashier before browsing |
| 3 | Pay exact cash or know change need |
| 4 | Keep stamped receipt until next bill |

Full bill workflow: [pay bills in Japan](/residents/pay-bills-japan). Utilities setup: [utilities guide](/residents/japan-utilities-guide).

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents misunderstand this">
  **The barcode expires.** Pay before the printed deadline. After expiry you may need a new slip from the company or ward office, not just a friendly cashier override.
</InfoBox>

### Daily-life scenario: first utility bill

You receive a yellow electricity slip in your mailbox. Amount ¥4,280, pay-by date the 27th. On the 25th you take the slip to Lawson, pay at the register in under 3 minutes, keep the stamped half in a kitchen folder.

## Printing and photocopy services

Multi-function machines (**マルチコピー機**) handle print, copy, scan, and sometimes photos.

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Print PDF** | USB, app, or email upload (chain-specific) |
| **Copy residence card** | Color copy, ¥10-¥50 per page |
| **Scan to email** | Select scan menu |
| **Photo print** | From phone app |

| Chain | Terminal name |
| --- | --- |
| 7-Eleven | Multi-copy machine |
| Lawson | Loppi |
| FamilyMart | FamiPort |

| Cost example | Yen |
| --- | --- |
| B&W A4 one page | ¥10-¥20 |
| Color A4 | ¥40-¥80 |
| Scan | ¥10-¥30 |

Bring a USB-C stick or install the chain app before an urgent ward-office print run.

## Ticket machines and event tickets

**Loppi** (Lawson) and **FamiPort** (FamilyMart) sell event, cinema, and some travel tickets. 7-Eleven uses its multi-copy / ticket flow.

| Ticket type | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Concert / sports** | Loppi, FamiPort |
| **Cinema** | All major chains |
| **Highway bus** | Lawson common |
| **Museum timed entry** | Varies |

| Flow | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Search event on terminal |
| 2 | Pay at register after reservation prints |
| 3 | Collect ticket slip |

Language is mostly Japanese. For high-demand events, practice on a low-stakes cinema booking first.

## Government document printing with My Number cards

Residents with a **My Number card (マイナンバーカード)** can print some certificates at convenience stores with a card reader, depending on municipality.

| Document | Konbini possible? |
| --- | --- |
| **Residence certificate (住民票)** | Many wards yes |
| **Tax certificate** | Some wards |
| **Seal registration** | Ward-specific |
| **Full visa paperwork** | Usually ward office only |

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| PIN | You set at issuance |
| Fee | ¥300-¥400 per copy typical |
| Reader | Card taps on machine |

Card setup: [My Number card guide](/residents/japan-my-number-card). Ward tasks: [city hall guide](/residents/japan-city-hall-guide).

  **Not every certificate is available at every store.** Check your ward’s official list before you assume the konbini can replace a city hall visit.
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Can I pay all bills at any convenience store?

Most utility barcodes list accepted chains. Read the slip footer.

### Do convenience stores accept credit cards for bill payment?

Usually **cash only** for konbini bill payment. Food and goods accept cards at many stores.

### Which ATM works best for foreign cards?

Seven Bank at 7-Eleven is the most common answer. Check your card issuer fees.

### How do I print a PDF from my phone?

Install the chain app (Seven Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart) and follow upload steps.

### Can I pick up Amazon packages at konbini?

Yes via Amazon Hub and counter services. Register your preferred store in the Amazon app.

### Are konbini certificates official?

Yes when your ward supports them. They are the same as city hall copies for supported docs.

### Is fax still necessary in 2026?

Less than before, but housing and some forms still ask. Know your nearest fax-capable store.

### What if I lose the payment slip?

Contact the utility or check email / customer portal for a reissue.

### Can I pay resident tax at konbini?

Yes, installments usually include barcodes. See [taxes guide](/residents/japan-taxes-guide).

### Is store Wi-Fi safe?

Fine for maps and messages. Avoid banking on public Wi-Fi.

### Why do clerks scan barcodes before I shop?

Payment codes must be processed at register before other items.

### Which chain is best for residents?

The one closest to home that accepts your usual bill barcodes.
