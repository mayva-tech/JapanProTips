## FAQ

### Do I need a point card to shop?

No. **Full price cash or IC** always works.

### Which point system is best for residents?

Depends where you shop. **Match app to your nearest supermarket and konbini.**

### Are Rakuten points worth it?

Worth it if you use **Rakuten services** already. Otherwise optional.

### Can foreigners register?

Yes with **Japanese phone number** usually. Rules vary by app.

### Should I keep every receipt?

No. Keep **large purchases and warranty** items only.

### What does staff ask at checkout?

Often **point card, receipt, bag**. Learn three short phrases.

### Do points expire?

Often **yes** after 1-2 years. Check app.

### Is PayPay the same as point cards?

PayPay is **payment plus bonuses**. Separate from supermarket store points unless linked campaign.
