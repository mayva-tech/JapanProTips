## Why business hours in Japan confuse foreign residents

| Expectation (abroad) | Japan common pattern |
| --- | --- |
| **Banks open late** | Close mid-afternoon |
| **Shops open late** | Many close 20:00-21:00 |
| **Sunday everything open** | More closed than you expect |
| **Holiday = slow day** | Holiday = fully closed |
| **24/7 phone support** | Weekday daytime default |

Hours are **predictable once mapped**, painful when discovered at the door.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Assuming one late-weekday block solves all errands. Japan rewards **morning Saturday planning** and **checking calendars**.

**Missed-deadline scenario:** Friday you need a bank stamp for housing. Branch closes 15:00. You arrive 15:20. Ticket machine off. You reschedule Monday and risk missing landlord deadline. You learn bank hours before life admin.

## FAQ

### What time do banks close in Japan?

Branch counters often close around 15:00 weekdays. ATMs differ.

### Are shops open on Sundays?

Many yes in cities. Small towns vary.

### Is everything closed on national holidays?

Banks and government yes. Konbini and many restaurants in cities open.

### How long is New Year closed?

Often Dec 29 through Jan 3 for many services. Staggered reopening.

### What is last order?

Last time kitchen takes food orders before closing.

### Can I do ward office tasks online?

Some cities yes. Many still need in-person first time.

### Why do cafes close so early?

Rent, labor, neighborhood rules. Chains stay later.

### How do residents plan Golden Week?

Travel early, cash, food, documents before cluster.

### Are post offices 24h?

Konbini post boxes yes. Counter hours limited.

### What if I miss a deadline because closed?

Document attempt, contact office next open day, ask extension calmly.
