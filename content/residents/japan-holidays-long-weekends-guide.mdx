## Why holidays in Japan surprise foreign residents

| Expectation | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **One day off means quiet** | Whole regions travel that day |
| **Shops stay open** | Many close New Year and Obon |
| **Book trains last minute** | Reserved seats sell out |
| **Office closed equals vacation mood** | Some staff still work remote |
| **Holidays are scattered** | Clusters create mega weeks |
| **Ward office open** | Often closed on holidays |

Holidays here are **national rhythm**, not personal background noise.

## How Japanese public holidays work

Japan uses **national holidays (国民の祝日)** set by law. When a holiday falls on Sunday, the next weekday is often a **substitute holiday (振替休日)**.

| Mechanism | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Fixed date holidays** | Same calendar date yearly |
| **Happy Monday system** | Some holidays move to Monday |
| **Bridge days** | Companies may grant extra days |
| **Substitute holiday** | Monday off if Sunday was holiday |

## Golden Week explained

**Golden Week (GW)** stacks late April and early May holidays into one of Japan's busiest travel periods.

| Date band | Holiday |
| --- | --- |
| Apr 29 | Showa Day |
| May 3 | Constitution Memorial Day |
| May 4 | Greenery Day |
| May 5 | Children's Day |

| What happens | Resident impact |
| --- | --- |
| **Domestic travel peak** | Shinkansen standing-room feel |
| **Hotels 2-3x price** | Book months ahead |
| **Popular sites packed** | Disney, Kyoto, Okinawa |
| **Some offices closed full week** | Company dependent |
| **Foreign tourism overlap** | Airports busy |

**Scenario:** Resident waits until April 15 to book Osaka trip for May 4-6. Hotels near station sold out. Train is unreserved only, 90 minutes standing.

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents underestimate this">
  **Golden Week is not "a long weekend."** It is closer to a nationwide migration. Treat it like Christmas week in the US or Lunar New Year in East Asia.
</InfoBox>

## Obon explained

**Obon (お盆)** is a summer period when many people return to family graves and hometowns. Exact dates vary by region (mid-August vs mid-July in some areas).

| Aspect | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Spiritual tradition** | Honor ancestors |
| **Travel direction** | Cities empty somewhat, highways busy outbound then return |
| **Corporate leave** | Many take Aug 13-16 block |
| **Festivals** | Bon odori in neighborhoods |

| Region style | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| **Tokyo style (新盆)** | Often around Aug 13-16 |
| **Kansai some areas** | July Obon in places |

Trains and expressways spike **before and after** the core days.

## New Year shutdown culture

**Oshogatsu (正月)** from late December to early January is the deepest annual pause.

| Period | What closes or slows |
| --- | --- |
| **Dec 29-31** | Banks, many clinics, shipping |
| **Jan 1-3** | Most offices, some restaurants |
| **First week January** | Gradual reopening |

| Still open | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Convenience stores** | Limited hours |
| **Some chain restaurants** | Fast food |
| **Hospitals** | Emergency only often |
| **Trains** | Reduced service Jan 1 |

| Resident task | Plan ahead |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | ATM lines Jan 1-2 |
| Ward office | Closed, file before Dec 25 |
| Packages | Delivery pauses |
| Grocery stock | Buy Dec 30 morning |

## Silver Week and long weekends

When holidays align with weekends, Japan gets **Silver Week** or random **3-4 day breaks**.

| Example pattern | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Saturday + Sunday + holiday Monday | 3-day trip boom |
| Equinox + Respect for Aged + weekend | Silver Week (rare years) |
| Substitute Monday after Sunday holiday | Extra bridge |

| Long weekend effect | Where you feel it |
| --- | --- |
| Friday night highways | 2-3 hour delays |
| Monday evening return | Packed trains |
| Domestic flights | Price jump |

Check the year's calendar in **September** especially. Silver Week is not every year.

## FAQ

### How many public holidays does Japan have per year?

Usually around 15 to 16 national holiday days, plus substitute holidays when dates align.

### Is Golden Week a good time to visit Kyoto?

It is lively but crowded and expensive. Residents often leave the city or avoid sightseeing cores.

### Do I get paid on public holidays?

Employees on standard contracts often receive holiday pay or day off. Check your contract. Part-time rules vary.

### Are trains running on January 1?

Yes but reduced frequency. Reserved seats fill early.

### What is Obon vs Marine Day?

Marine Day is a national holiday in July. Obon is a cultural travel season often in August, not always one legal holiday day.

### Can I renew my visa during Golden Week?

Immigration and ward offices are usually closed. Plan before.

### Is Silver Week every September?

No. It happens only when holidays and weekends align in specific years.

### Are convenience stores open on New Year's Day?

Most are, with possible shorter hours.

### Should residents travel domestically during GW?

Only if you booked early and accept crowds. Many prefer international trips booked months ahead or stay home.

### Do holidays affect garbage collection?

Yes. Wards publish holiday schedules. Check city website.

### Are theme parks open during holidays?

Yes, and they are packed.

### How do I find the official holiday calendar?

Search for the Cabinet Office **国民の祝日** calendar for the current year.
