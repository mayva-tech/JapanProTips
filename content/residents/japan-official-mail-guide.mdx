## Quick answer: Why official mail feels stressful

**Japan still uses paper for high-trust notices.** Kanji, red stamps, and registered mail look scary before you learn the patterns.

| Fear | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Unreadable envelope | Logo + a few key words matter |
| Red stamp | Official mark, not automatic fine |
| Missed deadline | Real consequences, but often fixable |
| Throwing away "junk" | May be tax or insurance |

**Quick answer:** Open mail within 48 hours, photograph every page, note any date in red, pay or respond before that date.

## Common types of official mail

### Official letter types table

| Mail type | Typical sender | What it often is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ward / city office** | 区役所 / 市役所 | Residence, insurance, child services |
| **Tax office** | 税務署 | Resident tax, national tax |
| **Pension** | 年金 | Enrollment, statements, changes |
| **Health insurance** | 国保 / 健保 | Premiums, cards, renewal |
| **Utilities** | Electric, gas, water | Bills, setup, safety checks |
| **Registered mail** | Japan Post | ID cards, PIN, bank tokens |
| **Landlord / management** | 管理会社 | Rules, inspections, warnings |

### Common senders table

| Sender look | Resident action |
| --- | --- |
| Ward logo on window envelope | Open same day |
| 税務署 or 住民税 | Calendar deadline |
| 日本年金機構 | Read, file or confirm employer handled |
| Utility company name | Pay or set auto-debit |
| Plain white, no logo | Still open; may be bank or employer |

## Red warning letters

| Visual cue | Urgency |
| --- | --- |
| Red text 至急 | Open today |
| 期限 / 締切 date | Hard deadline |
| 督促 | Follow-up, still fixable if you act |
| Registered 本人限定 | ID required at pickup |

### Urgency levels table

| Level | Examples | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open today** | Tax installment, registered ID mail | Same day |
| **This week** | Utility bill, insurance premium | Pay or schedule |
| **Read and file** | Pension statement, annual notice | Photo and folder |
| **Informational** | Newsletters, optional surveys | Read when calm |

## FAQ

### Is official mail always in Japanese?

Almost always. Use translation apps and ward English desk if available.

### What is registered mail (本人限定受取郵便)?

Must show ID to receive. Often cards or PINs.

### Can I pay official slips at konbini?

Many yes, if barcode is present.

### What if I miss a tax deadline?

Contact ward tax office quickly. Late fees may apply.

### Is pension mail urgent?

Read promptly. Employer enrollment may already cover you.

### How do I tell scam from real mail?

Real mail has your name, ward logo, paper slip. Scams push SMS links.
