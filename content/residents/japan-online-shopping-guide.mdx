## Why online shopping in Japan feels different

| Abroad habit | Japan reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Leave at door always** | Building may forbid it |
| **Anytime delivery OK** | Time slot required |
| **English checkout** | Often Japanese only |
| **One Amazon account** | Amazon.co.jp is separate |
| **Returns are easy** | Slower, stricter packaging |
| **Points are minor** | Rakuten ecosystem is huge |

You are joining **logistics culture**, not only clicking buy.

## The biggest online shopping platforms explained

### Amazon Japan

**Amazon.co.jp** is the default for speed, English UI option, and Prime delivery in cities.

| Strength | Weakness |
| --- | --- |
| Fast shipping tiers | Seller quality varies (marketplace) |
| English account settings | Not every item ships to your area |

| Locker and pickup points | Prime fee annual |

## Japanese address formatting problems

Couriers reject orders when the address does not match **official registration format**.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Postal code** | 7 digits, hyphen optional |
| **Prefecture** | 東京都, 大阪府, etc. |
| **City/ward** | 渋谷区, 横浜市 |
| **Chome-ban-go** | 3-12-5 style |
| **Building name** | Katakana often required |
| **Room number** | 101, not "first floor" |

| Mistake | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Romaji only | Delay or return |
| Wrong chome order | Driver calls, fails |
| Missing building name | Cannot find tower |
| Old address after move | Package to stranger |

Copy address from **residence certificate** or ward office printout. Full guide: [Japanese address system](/residents/japan-address-system).

## Delivery scheduling culture

Japan expects you to **choose a window** and be available (or authorize drop).

| Carrier style | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Yamato (Kuroneko)** | SMS link to reschedule |
| **Sagawa** | Similar redelivery flow |
| **Japan Post** | Hold at post office option |
| **Amazon Logistics** | Photo proof, locker option |

| Slot type | Typical hours |
| --- | --- |
| Morning | 8:00-12:00 |
| Afternoon | 12:00-14:00 or 14:00-16:00 |
| Evening | 16:00-18:00, 18:00-20:00, 19:00-21:00 |

**Scenario:** Office worker orders washer. Selects Saturday 14:00-16:00. Must answer intercom or leave **宅配ボックス** authorization with building manager first.

## FAQ

### Can I use Amazon US to ship to Japan?

Sometimes, but slow, duty risk, and plug issues. Amazon.co.jp is simpler for residents.

### Does Amazon Prime Japan work for foreigners?

Yes if you pay and live at a Japan address. Content streaming differs from US Prime Video.

### Is Rakuten worth it without Rakuten Card?

Yes on sale days. Card adds extra point percent.

### Can I buy on Mercari without Japanese?

App has some English. Seller messages may be Japanese only.

### What is cash on delivery?

You pay the driver or konbini when the package arrives. Fee about ¥300-¥600 sometimes.

### Why did my package return to sender?

Failed delivery attempts, wrong address, or unpaid konbini prepayment.

### Can packages be left at the door?

Only if building allows. Many mansions forbid unattended delivery.

### How do I change delivery after ordering?

Use SMS link from Yamato, Sagawa, or seller tracking page.

### Are returns free on Amazon Japan?

Often yes for Amazon-sold items. Third-party shops vary.

### Can I use a foreign credit card?

Sometimes on Amazon. Rakuten and Mercari often prefer Japan-issued cards.

### What is the package size limit for konbini pickup?

Roughly shoebox to small microwave depending on store. Large items need home delivery.

### Do I pay import tax on Amazon Japan?

Domestic Amazon orders usually include no import duty. Overseas sites are different.
