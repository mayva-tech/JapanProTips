## Why apartment soundscapes feel different in Japan

| Factor | Sound effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Thin walls in many units** | Neighbor life audible |
| **Train proximity** | Vibration and melody |
| **Dense neighborhoods** | Street activity close |
| **Scheduled public services** | Garbage, school bells |
| **Intercom culture** | Buzzers and announcements |
| **Seasonal open windows** | Outdoor sound enters |

Sound is part of **orientation**, not background only.

**Foreign residents slowly realize this:** Quiet does not mean silent. **Structured daily noise** is normal in cities.

## FAQ

### Are Japanese apartments always noisy?

No. **Varies by building age, materials, and location**. Train proximity matters most for many complaints.

### How do I check noise before renting?

Visit **weekday morning and evening**. Open windows. Walk near tracks.

### Is train noise unbearable?

Many residents adapt. **Earplugs and habituation** help. Some move farther from tracks.

### What is the garbage truck music?

A **ward-specific melody** announcing collection. Learn your days.

### Can I complain about neighbors?

Yes, often through **management (管理会社)** politely first.

### Do cicadas last all summer?

Peak is **hot weeks**. Volume drops after season turn.

### Why is the intercom so loud?

Building design. **Volume knob** sometimes in unit panel.

### Are political vans allowed at night?

Rules vary. **Daytime is common**. Close windows.
