## Why Japan's seasons feel different when living here

| Tourist view | Resident view |
| --- | --- |
| Cherry photos | Hay fever medicine budget |
| One rainy day | Three weeks of damp laundry |
| Hot vacation | Six weeks of sleep loss without AC |
| Snow postcard | High electricity and dry air |
| Four seasons poem | Four wardrobe overhauls |

You experience **repetition**, not highlights.

Month-by-month reference: [Japan weather by month](/guides/japan-weather-by-month).

## Spring realities

### Pollen

| Pollen type | Peak | Symptom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sugi (cedar)** | Feb-Apr (region dependent) | Eyes, nose, fatigue |
| **Hinoki** | Follows sugi | Similar |
| **Urban dust** | Year-round baseline | Mask helps |

Masks, antihistamines, and laundry dried indoors become normal. First spring surprises newcomers who never had allergies before.

### School and work transitions

April is **new fiscal year**: job transfers, school starts, apartment moves. Crowded trains return full force.

### Crowded season

Hanami parks and Golden Week travel spike prices. Book trains early if you travel.

| Spring cost (single, Tokyo, illustrative) | Monthly extra |
| --- | --- |
| Allergy meds | ¥2,000-¥5,000 |
| Tissues and masks | ¥1,000 |
| Heating off, AC not yet | Lower electric |

## Rainy season realities

**Tsuyu** (梅雨) usually June to mid-July in Kanto, earlier in Okinawa, later in Tohoku.

| Effect | What residents do |
| --- | --- |
| **Constant humidity** | Dehumidifier or AC dry mode |
| **Laundry won't dry** | Bathroom dryer, laundromat |
| **Mold risk** | Ventilate when rain pauses |
| **Sticky floors** | Closet moisture absorbers |

Tourist-focused detail: [rainy season in Japan](/guides/japan-rainy-season-guide).

**Scenario:** Week of rain, 1K apartment. Clothes smell musty without bathroom heater-dryer cycle. You buy ¥300 desiccant boxes for closet.

## Summer humidity and heat

| Challenge | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Humidity 70%+** | Sweat does not evaporate |
| **Nights warm** | Sleep quality drops |
| **Crowded heat islands** | Tokyo asphalt radiates |
| **Mosquitoes** | Screen doors, repellent |

Heatstroke risk is real. Carry water, use shade, know cooling centers in heat alerts.

## Air conditioning and electricity costs

Most residents run **AC daily** June through September.

| Setting | Comfort vs cost |
| --- | --- |
| **28°C cool** | Balanced |
| **24°C** | High bill |
| **Fan plus AC** | Better feel at 27-28°C |
| **Dry mode** | Rainy season mold fight |

**Summer electric (1K Tokyo, illustrative):**

| Month | Electric only |
| --- | --- |
| May | ¥4,000-¥6,000 |
| July | ¥10,000-¥16,000 |
| August | ¥12,000-¥18,000+ |

Full utilities context: [utilities guide](/residents/japan-utilities-guide).

## Typhoon season explained realistically

Typhoons hit **August through October** most years, not every week.

| Stage | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Forecast watch** | Stock water and food 1 day |
| **Strong wind** | Bring balcony items inside |
| **Train pause** | Check JR app, work from home |
| **After pass** | Check leaks, mold spots |

Apartments are concrete and generally safe indoors. **Balcony chairs fly**. Low areas flood.

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents get surprised by">
  **Convenience stores stay open** through many storms. ATMs and trains stop first.
</InfoBox>

## Autumn lifestyle changes

| Shift | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Comfortable air** | Best hiking months |
| **Typhoon tail** | Still possible Oct |
| **Heating off** | Open windows |
| **Seasonal konbini food** | Sweet potato, chestnut snacks |

October-November is when residents **recover socially** from summer exhaustion.

## Winter apartment realities

Japanese apartments often have **thin walls and single-pane feel** even with modern glass.

| Issue | Feeling |
| --- | --- |
| **Indoor 12-16°C** without heat | Cold at desk |
| **Bathroom cold** | Heated toilet seat luxury |
| **South-facing sun** | Helps afternoon |
| **North-facing** | Dark and chilly |

**Scenario:** January evening in 1K, heat with **AC unit on heat mode** or electric panel. Electric bill rises, toes still cold near window.

## Insulation and heating surprises

| Heat type | Common in apartments |
| --- | --- |
| **AC heat mode** | Most common, dry air |
| **Electric floor panel** | Kotatsu area |
| **Kerosene fan heater** | Rare in urban new builds |
| **City gas floor heating** | Premium units |
| **No central heating** | Normal |

Drafts from **balcony doors** and **entrance genkan** suck heat out.

## Laundry and drying challenges

| Season | Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rainy | No dry | Bathroom dryer |
| Summer | Quick dry outside | Pollen and dust |
| Winter | Indoor freeze-dry slow | Laundromat large dryers |
| Typhoon | Cannot hang balcony | Plan ahead |

Coin laundry **large dryers** (¥100-¥200 for 30 min) save marriages.

## Mental and physical adjustment over the year

| Season | Common mood |
| --- | --- |
| Spring | Busy, allergic, hopeful |
| Rainy | Low energy, mold stress |
| Summer | Irritable if hot sleep |
| Autumn | Relief, social |
| Winter | Indoor isolation risk |

Light, exercise, and **fixed sleep AC budget** help. Summer insomnia is a real health issue, not weakness.

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- No **dehumidifier plan** for first rainy season
- Running AC at 22°C all summer (bill shock)
- Leaving **balcony laundry** out in typhoon
- Winter with **thin socks only** at home
- Ignoring **mold on window tracks**
- Opening windows during pollen peak
- Assuming home country winter coat is enough (wrong humidity cut)

## What residents are surprised by

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents get surprised by">
  - **October can still feel like summer** in Tokyo
  - **Indoor and outdoor same humidity** without AC dry mode
  - **Gas stations sell kerosene** in rural areas, rare in city towers
  - **Oden season** is a cultural clock
  - **First typhoon** is boring indoors, scary on balcony video
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Do I need air conditioning in Tokyo?

Yes for summer sleep and health. Rare exceptions for hardy people.

### How bad is rainy season?

Manageable with dryer strategy. Three weeks feel long.

### Are typhoons dangerous in high-rise apartments?

Usually safe inside. Avoid windows and balconies in wind.

### Why is my apartment cold in winter?

Poor insulation, single-room heating. Normal for older units.

### Is pollen really that bad?

Many residents develop symptoms. Masks help.

### Should I dry laundry outside?

Fine in dry seasons. Risky in rain and typhoon.

### When is cheapest electric month?

Spring and autumn shoulder months often lowest.

### Do I need a humidifier in winter?

Sometimes for dry AC heat air. Summer needs dehumidify.

### Can I survive without Heattech?

Yes, but your heating bill and comfort suffer.

### Is March cold or warm?

Variable. Coat and light layer both.
