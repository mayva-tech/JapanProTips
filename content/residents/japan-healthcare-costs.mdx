## Quick answer: Is healthcare expensive in Japan?

**With valid public insurance, routine care is often affordable compared to many Western systems.** You usually pay roughly **10-30% copay** for covered services, not full private prices.

| Without insurance card | With insurance enrolled |
| --- | --- |
| Full self-pay rates | Copay at visit |
| Higher pharmacy bill | Standard copay share |
| Stress at counter | Predictable range |

**Quick answer:** Enroll in insurance, carry your card, expect a separate pharmacy stop. Most residents say "that was cheaper than expected" for basic visits.

## Typical clinic consultation costs

Illustrative **with insurance (30% copay)**. Actual bills vary.

### Clinic cost table

| Service | Typical cost with insurance |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic clinic visit** | About ¥500-¥1,500 copay |
| **Cold or flu consultation** | About ¥1,000-¥3,000 copay total visit |
| **Prescription medication** | About ¥500-¥2,000+ copay at pharmacy |
| **Dental cleaning** | Often ¥1,000-¥5,000+ (coverage limited) |
| **Specialist visit** | About ¥1,500-¥5,000+ copay plus tests |

High-cost care can hit **monthly out-of-pocket caps** for eligible households. Ask your municipality.

| Service | Insurance note |
| --- | --- |
| **Cleaning** | Partial or self-pay common |

| Topic | Resident experience |

| **Referral letter (紹介状)** | Helps booking at hospitals |

| **Department choice** | You may self-navigate |

<InfoBox title="Most foreign residents misunderstand this">
  - **"I need one family doctor forever."** Japan often uses clinics by symptom, not one assigned GP.
  - **"Insurance covers everything."** Dental, cosmetic, some tests differ.
  - **"ER is like US emergency pricing."** Different system; still use wisely.
  - **"Pharmacy is inside clinic."** Often a separate shop next door.
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Is healthcare expensive in Japan with insurance?

Routine visits are often modest copays. Major care can add up but caps may apply.

### How much is a doctor visit in Japan?

Often roughly ¥500-¥3,000 copay for common outpatient visits with insurance. Varies widely.

### Do I need insurance to see a doctor?

You can self-pay without insurance but costs are higher. Residents should enroll.

### Is dental covered?

Partially for some treatment. Cleaning and cosmetic often cost more out of pocket.

### Why is pharmacy separate?

System design. Clinic diagnoses, pharmacy dispenses.

### Can I use credit card at clinics?

Sometimes. Carry cash as backup.

### What if I forget my insurance card?

You may pay full price and reclaim later with paperwork. Ask clinic.

### Are English-speaking doctors available?

In cities, yes. Translation apps help elsewhere.

### How do specialists work?

Referral helps at hospitals. Some specialists take direct booking.

### Is ER expensive?

Can be costly. Use for true emergencies. Night clinics for moderate issues.

### Does hay fever medicine cost a lot?

Often affordable with insurance during allergy season.

### Where do I enroll in insurance?

City hall or employer HR. See NHI guide.
