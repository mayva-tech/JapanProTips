## Quick answer: Which phone plan should residents choose?

**Match network quality where you sleep and work, then optimize price.** Many residents use MVNO or Rakuten on WiFi-heavy lifestyles. Remote workers need stable indoor signal.

| Profile | Typical pick |
| --- | --- |
| Minimize cost, OK with app support | Rakuten Mobile or IIJmio-style MVNO |
| Balance price and reliability | UQ mobile, Y!mobile, povo |
| Maximum shop hand-holding | docomo, SoftBank, au flagship |
| Short stay | Prepaid or month-to-month MVNO |

**Quick answer:** If home fiber exists, do not pay for unlimited phone data you will not use. Pair [apartment internet](/residents/japan-apartment-internet) with a modest mobile plan.

Visitor SIMs: [tourist SIM guide](/guides/sim-card-japan). eSIM crossover possible on some carriers.

## Carrier comparison: docomo, SoftBank, au, MVNO

### AU vs SoftBank vs Docomo vs MVNO table

| Provider | Type | Typical monthly (single line) | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **docomo** | Big three | ¥7,000-¥11,000+ | Coverage, shops, corporate bundles | Expensive flagship |
| **SoftBank** | Big three | ¥7,000-¥11,000+ | Urban coverage, promotions | Complex discount rules |
| **au (KDDI)** | Big three | ¥7,000-¥11,000+ | Strong network, sub-brands | Same lock-in patterns |
| **UQ mobile / Y!mobile** | Sub-brand | ¥4,000-¥8,000 | au/SoftBank radio, retail | Still contract tricks |
| **Rakuten Mobile** | Own network | Often low with campaigns | Cheap data, app flow | Coverage pockets vary |
| **IIJmio, mineo, etc.** | MVNO | ¥2,000-¥5,000 | Low cost, SIM-only | Thinner support, caps |

## Contract traps

| Trap | What to read |
| --- | --- |
| **Residual device payment** | Phone installment separate from plan |
| **Early termination** | Penalty + lost rebates |
| **Auto-renewed options** | Insurance, cloud storage |
| **Intro pricing** | Price after month 6-12 |
| **Family discount dependency** | Loses value if you leave alone |

## FAQ

### Can foreigners sign phone contracts in Japan?

Yes with valid ID and payment method. Rules vary by carrier.

### What is MNP?

Mobile number portability. Keep your number when switching carriers.

### Rakuten Mobile vs MVNO?

Rakuten runs its own network. MVNOs rent docomo/au/SoftBank lines.

### Prepaid vs postpaid?

Residents usually use postpaid billed monthly. Prepaid for short stays.

### Cancellation fees?

Common on subsidized handset plans. SIM-only MVNO often lighter.

### Does phone plan affect visa?

No direct link. You need legal residence for contracts.
