## Why apartment cleaning in Japan feels different

| Factor | Effect on cleaning |
| --- | --- |
| **High humidity summers** | Mold risk in bathrooms and closets |
| **Small floor area** | Dust concentrates quickly |
| **Unit bath (ユニットバス)** | One molded room to scrub |
| **Kitchen without strong hood** | Grease on walls and fan |
| **Tatami or vinyl floors** | Different tools per surface |
| **Thin walls** | Vacuum timing matters for neighbors |
| **Seasonal rain** | Laundry and drying add moisture indoors |

Cleaning here is less about perfection and more about **preventing damage** before the lease ends.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Assuming a small room stays clean because it looks tidy. In Japan, **moisture control** is half of cleaning.

**Scenario:** First rainy season in a 1K. You skip wiping the bathroom after showers. Pink spots appear on the silicone seal by week three. You buy mold spray and start a two-minute wipe habit.

## Humidity and mold realities

| Season | Risk |
| --- | --- |
| **Rainy season (June-July)** | Constant damp air |
| **Summer** | AC cool plus outdoor humidity |
| **Winter** | Condensation on cold windows |
| **Typhoon weeks** | Wet laundry indoors |

| Sign | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Black or pink spots on silicone** | Mold spray, dry, ventilate |
| **Musty closet** | Charcoal packs, leave door open |
| **Window condensation** | Wipe daily, use dry mode on AC |
| **Towels never dry** | Dehumidifier or bathroom fan longer |

**Humidity-management example:** After shower, run the bathroom fan 20 minutes, wipe the mirror and door track, leave the door open to the main room. On humid days, run **ドライ (dry)** on the AC for 30 minutes before bed.

Rainy day habits: [rainy day life in Japan](/residents/japan-rainy-day-lifestyle-guide). AC dry mode detail: [air conditioner guide](/residents/japan-air-conditioner-guide).

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Opening windows all day in summer without understanding **outdoor humidity**. Sometimes AC dry mode or a dehumidifier works better than fresh air.

## Bathroom cleaning culture in Japan

Most budget apartments have a **unit bath**: toilet, sink, and shower-tub in one plastic room.

| Task | Frequency |
| --- | --- |
| **Wipe shower walls after use** | Daily or most days |
| **Scrub tub ring** | Weekly |
| **Clean toilet and sink** | Weekly |
| **Wash floor and drain area** | Weekly |
| **Check exhaust fan filter** | Monthly |

| Tool | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Squeegee (水切り)** | Walls and glass |
| **Toilet brush** | Standard |
| **Bath sponge with handle** | Back and tub without bending much |
| **Mold spray** | Silicone and corners |

**Scenario:** Shared building hot water runs evening only. You shower at 11 p.m., forget the fan, sleep. Next morning the room smells damp. You set a phone reminder: fan plus wipe before leaving the bathroom.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Using only bleach on colored mold. Residents often use **kabi (mold) sprays** made for bathrooms, then dry the area.

## Kitchen grease and ventilation realities

Japanese rental kitchens are often **one or two burners** with a **weak range hood** or a fan that vents back into the room.

| Problem | Source |
| --- | --- |
| **Sticky wall behind stove** | Stir-fry oil without hood power |
| **Greasy fan filter** | Months of frying |
| **Smell in curtains** | Open kitchen in 1R layout |

| Habit | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Wipe stove after each cook** | Prevents baked-on grease |
| **Boil water with lid** | Less steam in 1R |
| **Clean fan filter monthly** | Fire and smell risk |
| **Take trash out often** | Summer smell fast |

Cooking context: [home cooking in Japan](/residents/japan-home-cooking-guide). Trash timing: [trash and recycling](/residents/japan-trash-recycling-guide).

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Frying daily in a 1R without ventilation upgrades. Many residents shift to **grilling, boiling, or eating out** on work nights.

## Small apartment dust buildup

Less floor area does not mean less dust. **Fabric, tatami, and street pollen** collect in corners you see from the bed.

| Zone | Dust magnet |
| --- | --- |
| **Window tracks** | Pollen and soot |
| **Under bed** | Fiber and hair |
| **AC intake** | Filter clogs faster |
| **Entrance genkan** | Shoes and outside grit |

| Tool | Budget tip |
| --- | --- |
| **Compact stick vacuum** | Worth buying if lease is 1+ year |
| **Handheld duster** | 100-yen extendable type |
| **Microfiber cloths** | Wash weekly |

**Scenario:** Sunday 30-minute reset. Vacuum main path, wipe window ledge, shake entryway mat outside, empty mini trash. Apartment feels larger for the week.

## Tatami and flooring maintenance basics

| Floor type | Care |
| --- | --- |
| **Tatami (畳)** | Dry wipe, no wet mop soaking |
| **Vinyl / flooring (フローリング)** | Damp mop, avoid scratches |
| **Carpet (rare)** | Vacuum, stain spray |

| Tatami rule | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| **No shoes on tatami** | Dirt and oil stain |
| **Ventilate room** | Mold under tatami possible |
| **Lift light furniture occasionally** | Even wear |

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Steaming or soaking tatami like tile. Water damage can require **full tatami replacement** at move-out cost.

## Balcony cleaning and outdoor maintenance

Balconies hold **laundry, plants, and AC outdoor units**. They also collect pollen, pigeon droppings, and ash from neighbors who smoke.

| Task | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Sweep balcony floor** | Monthly |
| **Wipe railing** | Season change |
| **Check drain corner** | Before rainy season |
| **Clean laundry pole area** | When pollen heavy |

| Rule | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **No loud cleaning early morning** | Neighbor noise norms |
| **Water runoff** | Some buildings restrict hosing |
| **Drying laundry** | Shake mats outside carefully |

## Laundry and drying-space cleaning

Laundry adds **indoor humidity** when you cannot dry on the balcony.

| Setup | Cleaning note |
| --- | --- |
| **Bathroom dryer function** | Wipe lint and drum monthly |
| **Balcony lines** | Wash clothespins and lines |
| **Indoor rack** | Mold on drip tray if pooled water |
| **Washing machine** | Run tub clean cycle monthly |

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Wet clothes overnight in bathroom** | Finish dry or move to AC room |
| **Never cleaning washer drum** | 槽洗浄 (tub clean) tablets at drugstore |

## Air conditioner cleaning basics

Wall AC units need **filter cleaning** you do yourself in most rentals.

| Step | How often |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove and wash filters** | Every 2-4 weeks in summer |
| **Wipe intake area** | Monthly |
| **Professional deep clean** | Optional every few years |

Dirty filters raise bills and smell when heating starts.

Full guide: [air conditioners in Japan](/residents/japan-air-conditioner-guide).

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Ignoring filters until the unit smells. Five minutes under the shower stream fixes most summer issues.

## Drain smells and plumbing realities

| Smell source | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Kitchen sink** | Boiling water, baking soda rinse, clean trap if accessible |
| **Bathroom drain** | Drain cleaner granules (use as labeled) |
| **Washing machine drain** | Tub clean plus drain filter |
| **Toilet tank** | Standard cleaner, not mixed chemicals |

| Caution | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Do not mix bleach and acid cleaners** | Dangerous gas |
| **Hair in shower drain** | Buy small catch screen at drugstore |

**Scenario:** August smell from kitchen drain. You run hot water, use a drain powder packet from Don Quijote, and stop pouring oil down the sink.

| Area landlords check | Standard |
| --- | --- |

| **Flooring scratches** | Furniture drag marks |

## FAQ

### How often should I clean a small Japanese apartment?

Many residents do **daily wipes** (bathroom, kitchen) plus **weekly vacuum** and **monthly** deeper tasks (AC filter, fan).

### What is the best mold product in Japan?

**Kabi Killer** and similar bathroom mold sprays are common. Follow label, ventilate, and dry the area after.

### Can I use a steam mop on tatami?

No. Use dry or barely damp methods on tatami. Vinyl flooring tolerates damp mopping with care.

### Who pays for move-out cleaning?

Often the tenant via a **fixed cleaning fee** in the lease. Read your contract before you move.

### How do I stop bathroom mold on silicone?

Wipe after showers, run the fan, use mold spray early, and keep the door open after use.

### Is bleach safe for unit baths?

Many residents use labeled bathroom cleaners instead of mixing strong chemicals. Never mix bleach with other products.

### Do I need a dehumidifier?

Helpful in humid regions or rooms without good AC dry mode. Closet packs work for small spaces first.

### How do I clean the AC filter?

Open the front panel, slide filters out, rinse gently, dry fully, reinstall. See [air conditioner guide](/residents/japan-air-conditioner-guide).

### What if my drain smells bad?

Hot water flush, baking soda, or store drain cleaner as directed. Persistent smell: ask management (管理会社).
