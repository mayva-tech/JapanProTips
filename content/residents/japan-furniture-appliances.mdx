## Why furnishing an apartment in Japan surprises foreigners

| Surprise | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Unfurnished default** | You buy or rent furniture |
| **Narrow doors and stairs** | Large IKEA boxes may not fit |
| **No closet** in many units | Need wardrobe purchase |
| **Washer on balcony** | Plumbing type matters |
| **Disposal fees** when leaving | Appliances cost money to discard |
| **Cash vs card** at delivery | Carry both |

Plan for **setup week**, not setup afternoon.

## Apartment size realities in Japan

| Layout | Typical size mindset | Furnishing note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1K** | One room + kitchen niche | Bed, small table, minimal sofa |
| **1DK** | Room + dining-kitchen | Slightly more layout freedom |
| **1LDK** | Living + bedroom area | Sofa and desk realistic |
| **2LDK** | Family size | Full appliance set |

**Example 1K (25 m²):** Semi-double bed, Nitori folding table, wall shelf, compact fridge, combo washer-dryer if balcony allows. Skip large sofa.

**Example 1LDK (45 m²):** Bed, sofa-bed, TV stand, full-size fridge, separate washer, microwave, desk chair.

Measure **door width, elevator depth, balcony door** before buying.

## Best furniture stores in Japan

### Nitori

- Nationwide **budget** furniture and home goods
- Japanese room sizes in mind
- Delivery and assembly services
- English app limited; store maps help

### IKEA Japan

- **Flat-pack** familiar to many foreigners
- Larger items for 1LDK+
- Delivery slots book online
- Stores in Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya areas, online shipping elsewhere

### Muji

- Minimal design, storage, bedding, small furniture
- Higher aesthetic price point
- Good for compact organizers

### Local appliance chains

| Chain | Strength |
| --- | --- |
| **Yamada Denki, Bic Camera, Kojima** | Sales bundles, points |
| **Cainz, Konan** (home centers) | Suburban access |

Compare **delivery + installation** bundle on washers.

## Secondhand stores explained

### Hard Off / Book Off group

Electronics and appliances tested resale.

### Second Street

Furniture and clothing resale chains.

### Jimoty (community marketplace)

Local pickup deals, Japanese interface, cash often.

| Source | Price | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Second Street bed frame | Low | Check bed bugs policy, clean well |
| Jimoty washer | Very low | No warranty, verify works |
| Nitori new mattress | Mid | Hygiene safe |

## Washing machines and balcony setups

Japan apartments often use **balcony washer plumbing**.

| Type | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Outdoor hookup** | Standard washer drain to balcony |
| **Combo washer-dryer** | Good for 1K, expensive upfront |
| **Building coin laundry** | Skip washer purchase |
| **Indoor drain models** | Rare; verify plumbing |

Measure balcony **width and water tap** before delivery day.

| --- | --- | --- |

Microwave: **oven-range** combos popular but need ventilation space.

| Step | Detail |
| --- | --- |

| **Stairs carry** | Fee if no elevator |

## Recycling fees and appliance disposal laws

You **cannot leave appliances on street** like trash.

| Item | Disposal path |
| --- | --- |
| Large appliances | **Sodai gomi** (oversized waste) fee sticker from city or retailer take-back |
| Furniture | Sodai gomi booking or recycle shop |
| Moving out | Plan disposal when leaving |

Fees often **¥2,000-¥8,000+** per large item depending on municipality.

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Buying sofa before **measuring elevator**
- Washer that does not match **balcony plumbing**
- Ignoring **disposal cost** when buying huge items
- Missing delivery window and **redelivery fee**
- No **curtains** (morning sun brutal in summer)
- Throwing furniture in garbage pile (illegal)
- Buying US voltage appliances without **transformer plan**

## FAQ

### Should I buy IKEA or Nitori?

Nitori for small Japanese rooms. IKEA for larger layouts and familiar flat-pack.

### Can I leave furniture when I move out?

No. Dispose via sodai gomi, sell on Jimoty, or hire removal service.

### Do apartments include washer and fridge?

Sometimes. Read lease carefully.

### Is futon or bed better for 1K?

Futon saves space. Bed improves sleep for many foreigners.

### Can delivery enter small elevator?

Measure first. Stores warn about carry-up fees.

### Are used washers safe?

Buy from reputable chains with testing. Jimoty is buyer beware.

### Do I need a Japanese phone number for delivery?

Helpful. Use employer or friend if needed at order.

### Can I use foreign appliances?

Japan is 100V. US devices sometimes work. EU 220V devices need transformers, often not worth it.

### When is best sale season?

New Year, Golden Week, Obon sales at appliance stores.

### Who installs air conditioning?

Often landlord or specialist. Not DIY in most rentals.
