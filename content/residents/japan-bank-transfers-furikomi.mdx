## Quick answer: What is furikomi?

**Furikomi is a domestic bank transfer** from your account to another Japanese bank account using branch codes, account number, and recipient name.

| Use case | How residents pay |
| --- | --- |
| Rent | Monthly furikomi to landlord account |
| Utilities | Auto-debit or furikomi |
| School fees | Transfer with reference field |
| Splitting costs | PayPay or furikomi |

**Quick answer:** Use your bank app after the first ATM lesson. Save rent as a registered payee.

## ATM vs app transfer comparison

| Method | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ATM (cash card hours)** | No smartphone needed | Kanji UI, branch hours |
| **Bank app** | Templates, English on some apps | Setup, daily limits |
| **Online banking PC** | Large typing for names | Less common mobile-first |
| **PayPay / wallet** | Person to person | Not landlord rent default |

## Transfer fee explanations

| Transfer type | Typical fee |
| --- | --- |
| Same bank | Often free |
| Other bank weekday | ¥110-¥330 common |
| After hours / Sunday | Higher tier possible |
| International | Separate SWIFT path |

## Account name issues

| Issue | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong katakana spacing | Transfer rejected |
| Full-width vs half-width | Error |
| Company name abbreviated | Must match bank record |
| Typo in account number | Bounce, investigate fast |

Copy payee details from lease or payment slip. Do not guess.

## Paying rent and landlord culture

| Norm | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Due date | Often by 25th or month end |
| Proof | Screenshot or receipt if asked |
| Auto-debit option | Some landlords offer |
| Management company account | Pay listed account only |

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Transfer after bank cut-off | Arrives next business day |
| Wrong branch code (店番) | Triple-check with slip |
| Using foreign name only | Use registered katakana |
| No balance for fee | Include fee in balance check |
| Phishing "rent change" email | Verify with management |

<InfoBox title="Most foreign residents misunderstand this">
  - **"ATM is the only way."** Apps are standard after setup.
  - **"Rent can wait a few days."** Late fees and trust issues possible.
  - **"Roman letters work on furikomi."** Katakana account name usually required.
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### What is furikomi in English?

Bank transfer / remittance within Japan.

### Can I furikomi without Japanese?

Some bank apps have English. ATM is harder. Bring a template photo.

### How long does transfer take?

Same bank often instant. Other bank same day or next business day.

### Furikomi vs PayPay?

Furikomi is bank to bank. PayPay is wallet P2P.

### Daily transfer limits?

Yes, set by bank and account type. Raise at branch if needed.
