## Quick answer: Clinic or hospital?

| Situation | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Cold, rash, mild fever, refill | **Clinic (クリニック / 診療所)** |
| Specialist tests, surgery prep | **Hospital (病院)** often with referral |
| Chest pain, severe injury, stroke signs | **Emergency (119)** |
| Night weekend moderate issue | **Night clinic (夜間急病)** before ER |

**Quick answer:** Search 内科 or symptom + クリニック near home. Carry insurance card.

Costs: [healthcare costs](/residents/japan-healthcare-costs). Insurance: [health insurance](/residents/japan-health-insurance).

## Why residents use clinics first

| Clinic advantage | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Faster intake | Shorter wait for simple issues |
| Neighborhood based | Same area when you are sick |
| Department labeled | 内科, 皮膚科, etc. |
| Lower stress | Smaller building |

Japan does not use one assigned GP everywhere. You choose clinics by symptom.

## Hospitals: when they fit

| Hospital use | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Referral from clinic | Orthopedics, ENT tests |
| Large imaging | MRI scheduled |
| Inpatient care | Admission |
| True emergency | Ambulance destination |

Big hospitals can mean **long waits** for minor complaints. Staff may redirect you to a clinic.

## Referral system confusion

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **紹介状 (shokaijo)** | Referral letter from clinic |
| **Direct booking** | Some specialists accept without |
| **University hospital** | Research hospitals, long waits |

<InfoBox title="Most foreign residents misunderstand this">
  - **"Hospital is always better."** Clinics handle most daily care.
  - **"ER is free with insurance."** You pay copay; use for emergencies.
  - **"One doctor for everything forever."** Clinic-by-symptom is normal.
</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Can I go to hospital without referral?

Sometimes, but wait may be long. Clinic first is standard.

### Clinic vs hospital cost difference?

Both use insurance copay. Hospital tests add fees.

### English-speaking clinic?

Exists in cities. Apps help elsewhere.

### What is 内科?

Internal medicine, general adult clinic start.

### Pediatric?

児科 clinics for children.
