## Why moving in Japan feels overwhelming

| Stress | Why it hits hard |
| --- | --- |
| **Peak season crunch** | March slots sell out |
| **Japanese-only forms** | Movers and utilities |
| **Thin walls, strict rules** | Building notices required |
| **Cash and timing** | Pay mover on day, keys at fixed hour |
| **Parallel paperwork** | Ward office plus contracts |
| **Trash and furniture** | Cannot leave sofa on street |

You are coordinating **logistics and bureaucracy** at once.

## Typical moving timeline in Japan

| Weeks before move | Task |
| --- | --- |
| **8-10** | Read lease exit rules, pick move date |
| **6-8** | Book mover or rent truck |
| **4-6** | Give landlord notice, start utilities transfer |
| **3-4** | Internet install appointment at new unit |
| **2** | Ward moving-out and moving-in paperwork |
| **1** | Pack, label boxes, confirm elevator slot |
| **Move week** | Clean old unit, key return, inspection |
| **After** | Update bank, phone, Amazon address |

**Scenario:** Move March 28. Notice given January 28. Mover booked February 5. Utilities switched March 20. Internet install March 27 (risky). Safer internet booking mid-February.

## Moving companies explained

**Hikkoshi (引越し)** companies range from full service to transport only.

| Service level | Includes |
| --- | --- |
| **Full pack** | Boxes, wrap, disassemble furniture |
| **Standard** | Carry, truck, basic protection |
| **Transport only** | You pack, they drive |
| **Single-item** | Washer, piano, AC unit |

| Known types | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Major chains** | Sakai, Art, ARROW, Nittsu network |
| **Budget brands** | Akabou style, smaller trucks |
| **Platform quotes** | Compare online, read reviews |

| What to ask in quote | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Distance and floor | Stairs cost extra |
| Elevator reservation | Building may require |
| Appliance disconnect | Washer, AC |
| Insurance cap | Damage limits |
| Payment method | Cash vs card |

## DIY moving vs professional movers

| Factor | DIY (rent k truck) | Professional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cost studio** | ¥15,000-¥35,000 | ¥40,000-¥90,000+ |
| **Cost 1LDK** | ¥25,000-¥50,000 | ¥60,000-¥120,000+ |
| **Labor** | You plus friends | Crew included |
| **Time** | Full day, exhausting | Faster |
| **Building rules** | Some ban DIY trucks | Company name on reservation |
| **Insurance** | Your risk | Company policy |
| **Japanese** | Rental contract JP | Staff often basic English in cities |

| Choose DIY when | Choose pros when |
| --- | --- |
| Studio, few items | Family, large furniture |
| Ground floor both sides | No friends available |
| Off-peak weekday | Peak season March |
| Tight budget | Building requires vendor |

## Peak moving season realities

| Season | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| **Late February to April** | Highest (school and fiscal year) |
| **August** | Student moves, moderate |
| **End of year** | Lower except holidays |
| **Rainy season** | Cheaper, wet carry risk |

| Peak effect | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Prices rise | 20-50% vs off-peak |
| Dates scarce | Book 4-8 weeks ahead |
| Elevator slots | Fight with neighbors |
| Ward office lines | Longer waits |

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents panic about this">
  **"I can book a mover two weeks before March 31."** Often false in Tokyo. Off-peak you might. Peak season needs **4-8 weeks** or flexible midweek dates.
</InfoBox>

## Utility transfer procedures

Utilities do not follow you automatically. You **cancel or transfer** by move date.

| Utility | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Electricity** | Call or web: end old, start new |
| **Gas** | Appointment for shutoff and startup |
| **Water** | City or provider notice |
| **Propane** | If applicable, strict timing |

| Transfer example | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Old electric ends | March 28 23:59 |
| New electric starts | March 29 00:00 |
| Final meter reading | Photo on move day |
| First new bill | About 4-6 weeks |

Full detail: [utilities in Japan](/residents/japan-utilities-setup).

| Mistake | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Forget gas shutoff | Billing continues |
| Overlap two homes 2 weeks | Double pay |
| No final reading photo | Deposit dispute |

## FAQ

### How much does a moving company cost in Japan?

Studio off-peak often ¥40,000-¥70,000. Peak season and lots of furniture push higher. Get quotes.

### When should I book movers for a March move?

Ideally **January or early February** for popular dates.

### Can I move without speaking Japanese?

In major cities, some crews have basic English. Ward office may need a friend or translator.

### Do I need to clean the apartment myself?

Often yes, unless you pay a move-out cleaning company or the lease requires one.

### How do I dispose of a bed or sofa?

Sodai gomi system in your ward. Buy sticker, book slot. Rules vary by city.

### What if internet is not ready at the new apartment?

Use pocket WiFi, phone tether, or cafe until install completes.

### Can foreigners use any moving company?

Yes. Payment and building rules matter more than nationality.

### How long does deposit return take?

Often 1-2 months after inspection. Disputes add time.

### Is DIY moving allowed in all buildings?

No. Check management rules. Some require registered vendors.

### Do I change address at city hall?

Yes when you change ward or municipality. See residence registration rules.

### What about mail after moving?

File forwarding (転送) at post office or update senders.

### Should I tip movers in Japan?

Not required. Small drinks or snacks are optional, not expected like US tips.
