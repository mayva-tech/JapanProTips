## Why utilities in Japan confuse foreign residents

| Confusion | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Separate companies** | Electric, gas, water, internet are not one bundled bill |
| **Japanese-only letters** | Bills and setup calls often Japanese first |
| **Move-in day gaps** | Power may be off until you call |
| **Gas safety visit** | Mandatory check before first use |
| **Seasonal spikes** | Summer AC and winter heat change budgets fast |
| **Building rules** | Some fees paid via landlord, not direct |

You are not behind if the first month feels like paperwork boot camp.

## The main utilities explained

### Electricity

Powers lights, AC, washer, fridge, TV, and most kitchen appliances.

| Topic | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Who bills you** | Retail electric company (after market deregulation) |
| **Unit** | kWh usage on monthly statement |
| **Apartment** | Usually tenant contracts directly |
| **Peak stress** | Summer AC |

### Gas

Powers stove, hot water in many units, and some heating.

| Type | Common in |
| --- | --- |
| **City gas (都市ガス)** | Urban apartments, piped |
| **LP gas (プロパン)** | Some older or rural units, tank delivery |

Gas requires **safety check** before activation.

### Water

Supplied to kitchen, bath, toilet. Sewage often on same municipal bill.

| Topic | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Billing** | City or ward water bureau |
| **Tenant vs landlord** | Lease says who pays |
| **Activation** | Sometimes automatic when electric starts |

### Internet

Not a government utility, but treated like one in monthly budgeting.

Fiber, cable, or mobile home routers. Deep setup guide: [internet and Wi-Fi in Japan apartments](/residents/japan-internet-setup).

## Utility setup after moving in

| Day | Task |
| --- | --- |
| **Move-in morning** | Confirm breaker on, locate gas meter and water main |
| **Day 1** | Call or web-start **electricity** in your name |
| **Day 1-2** | Schedule **gas** opening and safety visit |
| **Day 1-3** | Confirm **water** active (ask landlord if unsure) |
| **Week 1** | Book **internet** install (2-4 weeks lead time common) |
| **Week 2** | Set **auto-debit** or konbini payment habit |

**Setup timeline (typical Tokyo 1K):**

| Utility | Activation | First bill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electricity | Same day to 2 days | ~30-45 days later |
| Gas | Visit within 3-7 days | Next cycle |
| Water | Often already on | Monthly or bimonthly |
| Internet | Install 2-4 weeks | Starts after install |

Housing context: [renting an apartment in Japan](/residents/renting-apartment-japan).

## Electricity companies and deregulation

Japan **deregulated retail electricity**. You choose a provider but still use local grid infrastructure.

| Provider style | Examples (change over time) |
| --- | --- |
| **Major utilities** | TEPCO, Kansai Electric retail arms |
| **Alternate retailers** | Octopus, Looop, smaller green plans |

| Decision | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| **Default at move-in** | Start with simple plan, switch later |
| **English support** | Some retailers offer it; read contract PDF |
| **Contract length** | Check cancellation fee |

You need **address, move-in date, and payment method** to start.

## Gas company visits and safety checks

Before first flame use, staff run **開栓** (opening) and safety inspection.

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| You call gas company | Provide address, name, move-in date |
| Appointment window | 2-4 hour block, be home |
| Technician visit | Checks stove, hot water, leaks |
| Activation | You can cook same day after pass |

<InfoBox title="Foreign residents get confused by">
  **You cannot self-turn on city gas** before the visit. Skipping this step is unsafe and against procedure.
</InfoBox>

LP gas buildings use a different supplier number on the wall sticker. Read the meter label.

## Water activation realities

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Water already running** | Confirm with landlord; wait for first bill |
| **Name change needed** | Ward water desk or online portal (area dependent) |
| **Included in rent** | No separate signup; check lease |
| **Move-out** | Final reading scheduled |

Water bills are often **lower than electric** but still recurring.

## Internet installation timelines

| Method | Lead time | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fiber install** | 2-4 weeks | Long stays, heavy work from home |
| **Provider pre-wired building** | Days to 1 week | New towers |
| **Home router SIM** | Same day pickup | Short gap after move |
| **Pocket Wi-Fi** | Immediate | First 2 weeks only |

## Seasonal utility cost differences

| Season | Driver |
| --- | --- |
| **Summer (Jul-Sep)** | Air conditioning |
| **Winter (Dec-Feb)** | Heating, hot water, dry air devices |
| **Spring and fall** | Lowest months for many households |

Tokyo-area **single person, 1K** illustrative monthly totals (electric + gas + water, internet separate):

| Season | Low | Typical | High usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spring or fall | ¥6,000 | ¥8,000 | ¥10,000 |
| Summer | ¥9,000 | ¥12,000 | ¥18,000+ |
| Winter | ¥8,000 | ¥11,000 | ¥15,000 |

Add **¥4,000-¥6,000+** for home internet.

## Summer air conditioning costs

| Habit | Effect on bill |
| --- | --- |
| **28°C setpoint vs 22°C** | Large savings |
| **Closed curtains daytime** | Less heat load |
| **Clean filter monthly** | Better efficiency |
| **Fan plus AC** | Cool feel at higher temp |

**Example:** Same 1K, July bill ¥7,500 electric only vs ¥14,000 with AC all day at 23°C.

## Winter heating costs

| Heat type | Cost note |
| --- | --- |
| **AC heat mode** | Electric bill rises |
| **Gas floor heating (床暖房)** | Rare, pricey |
| **Kotatsu plus space heater** | Electric localized spikes |
| **Hot water usage** | Long showers show on gas |

Insulate with **door curtains** and **window film** where lease allows.

## Automatic payments and convenience store payments

| Method | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bank auto-debit (口座振替)** | Never miss date | Needs Japanese account |
| **Credit card auto-pay** | Points possible | Not all utilities accept |
| **Konbini barcode** | No bank needed | Manual each month |
| **Pay-easy / online banking** | Fast | Japanese UI |

Register auto-debit **after first bill** arrives with your customer number.

## Smart meters and usage tracking

Many areas now use **smart meters** for electricity.

| Feature | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Daily usage online** | Spot AC leaks early |
| **Hourly charts** | Shift laundry to off-peak if plan rewards it |
| **Estimated monthly** | Budget before bill shock |

Gas and water smart rollout varies by city.

## What happens if you miss payments

| Stage | Typical sequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Reminder notice** | Late fee may apply |
| **Second notice** | Stronger wording |
| **Service warning** | Electricity or gas cut risk |
| **Disconnection** | Rare but possible after prolonged non-payment |
| **Move-out** | Unpaid balance blocks clean handover |

Call the company **before** the due date if you know you will be late. Payment plans exist sometimes.

## Common mistakes foreign residents make

- Assuming previous tenant contract transfers automatically
- Missing **gas appointment** and eating only konbini for a week
- Ignoring **breaker off** after long vacancy
- Paying **landlord and utility twice** for water without reading lease
- Using pocket Wi-Fi six months (expensive)
- Throwing away **unpaid bill envelopes** thinking they are ads
- Not registering **My Page** and missing digital notices

## Apartment utility quirks in Japan

| Quirk | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **All-electric building** | No gas, induction stove |
| **Utilities included in rent** | Common in some shares |
| **Shared laundry meter** | Coin laundry only |

**Example 1K all-electric:** Electric only + water. No gas visit. Budget higher summer electric.

**Example older 1DK:** Gas stove, gas hot water, separate electric for AC and lights.

## FAQ

### Do I need a Japanese phone number to sign up?

Helpful. Some web forms accept email only.

### Can I start electricity before move-in?

Usually from move-in date, not weeks early.

### Who pays water in a share house?

Lease or house rules. Ask in writing.

### Is gas expensive?

Usage-based. Cooking daily is moderate; constant hot water baths cost more.

### Can I pay utilities from a foreign bank card?

Konbini cash is easiest without Japan bank. Auto-debit needs local account.

### What if I do not read Japanese bills?

Photograph and use translation app; learn the **due date** kanji 支払期限.

### Do I cancel utilities when moving out?

Yes, call each company with move-out date for final meter read.

### Are utilities in my name if landlord pays?

Sometimes landlord bills you monthly. Clarify to avoid double pay.

### How long until internet works?

Plan 2-4 weeks for fiber; have backup mobile data.

### Does earthquake affect billing?

After disasters, companies sometimes delay cuts or offer plans. Watch official notices.
