## Why paperwork piles up quickly in Japan

| Paper source | Why it multiplies |
| --- | --- |
| **Government notices** | Ward, tax, pension |
| **Utilities** | Monthly slips even with auto-debit |
| **Housing** | Lease, renewal, insurance |
| **Shopping** | Warranties, manuals |
| **Healthcare** | Receipts for reimbursement |
| **Banking** | Statements, contracts |

Japan still trusts **physical records** for many systems. Digital exists, but your apartment becomes the filing cabinet.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Keeping every paper forever. You need **categories and retention rules**, not a museum.

**Scenario:** Move-in week. You stuff all envelopes in one box. Six months later you need **lease copy for visa renewal**. You dig 40 minutes. You build labeled folders that weekend.

## Residence card and My Number document management

| Document | Storage tip |
| --- | --- |
| **Residence card (在留カード)** | Wallet when out, safe spot at home |
| **My Number notification** | Folder, not loose |
| **My Number card** | Separate from PIN letter |
| **PIN envelope** | Hidden, never with card |

Guides: [My Number Card](/residents/japan-my-number-card), [residence registration](/residents/japan-residence-registration).

| Security habit | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Never post photos of cards online | Fraud |
| Shred old copies with number | Privacy |
| Report loss immediately | Legal requirement |

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Storing My Number card in phone case with PIN note. **Separate storage** is standard advice.

## FAQ

### How long keep utility bills?

Often 6-12 months after payment confirmed. Keep contract longer.

### Do I need paper bank statements?

PDF download monthly is enough for many residents. Keep opening docs.

### Where store residence card?

Wallet when outside. Safe at home overnight. Not loose on desk.

### Can I throw appliance manuals?

Photo model and warranty. Recycle manual if space tight.

### What paperwork for visa renewal?

Varies by status. Income proof, residence, sometimes lease. Check immigration list early.

### How organize in 1K apartment?

One box, five folders, cloud backup. No filing cabinet needed.

### Should I keep all medical receipts?

Major ones and year-end tax related. Routine colds optional.

### How handle Japanese-only contracts?

Scan, translate key dates, ask bilingual friend or lawyer if high stakes.

### Is My Number paper special?

Yes. Treat like identity. Shred old copies.

### Digital copies legal enough?

Often yes for personal records. Some processes want originals.
