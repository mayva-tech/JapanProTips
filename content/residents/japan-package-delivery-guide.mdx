## Why package delivery in Japan surprises foreigners

| Expectation abroad | Japan reality |
| --- | --- |
| **Leave at door always** | Signature or locker common |
| **Anytime drop-off** | Time slot culture |
| **Missed = automatic retry** | You schedule redelivery |
| **Carrier does not matter** | Seller picks Yamato, Post, Sagawa |
| **Name on buzzer optional** | Mismatch causes failed delivery |

Delivery is a **system you configure once**, then maintain.

## Missed delivery slips explained

**不在票 (fuzaihyo)** is left when you are not available.

| Slip section | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Tracking number | Track online |
| QR code | Schedule redelivery |
| Phone number | Automated redelivery line |
| Date attempted | Reference |
| Depot location | Temporary hold |

| Scenario | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| You were at work | Normal slip |
| Intercom name wrong | Failed attempt, slip |
| No answer in 2 minutes | Driver moves on |
| Building locked | Slip or depot |

## FAQ

### What if I miss delivery twice?

Often returned to sender. Contact seller fast.

### Can I change address after shipping?

Sometimes before dispatch. Call seller support.

### Is Yamato the same as Kuroneko?

Yes. Yamato Transport uses the black cat brand.

### Do I need Japanese for redelivery?

QR slips work with minimal Japanese. Phone is harder.

### Can neighbors accept my package?

Sometimes with building rules. Risk if not arranged.

### How long is depot hold?

Often about one week. Check slip.

### Are delivery lockers free?

Usually free for pickup window. Late fees possible.

### Can I refuse a package?

Possible before signing. Contact seller for returns.

### Why no Sunday delivery sometimes?

Seller and carrier policy varies.

### Does Japan Post deliver on Saturday?

Often yes for Yu-Pack. Check tracking.

### What is okihai?

Authorized leave-at-location delivery. Register with carrier and building.

### Are fake delivery texts common?

Yes. Use apps, not SMS links. See scams guide.
