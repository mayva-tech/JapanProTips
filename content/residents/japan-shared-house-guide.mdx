## Why shared houses are popular in Japan

| Reason | Who it helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Lower move-in cost** | New arrivals |
| **Furniture included** | Short stays |
| **Flexible contracts** | Students, job changers |
| **Built-in social network** | Lonely first year |
| **English-friendly operators** | Foreign residents |
| **No traditional guarantor sometimes** | Visa newcomers |

Tokyo and Osaka have **dozens of operator brands** plus independent houses.

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Thinking shared house equals cheap forever. **Convenience has a monthly premium** over bare apartments in some areas.

## What a shared house actually is

| Element | Typical setup |
| --- | --- |
| **Private bedroom** | Lockable door |
| **Shared kitchen** | Fridges labeled |
| **Shared bathrooms** | 1 per 3-8 people |
| **Living room** | TV, table, sofa |
| **Utilities included** | In rent often |
| **Wi-Fi included** | Often house-wide |
| **Manager** | On-site or remote |

| Not usually included | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Your own washer** | Shared machine |
| **Parking** | Rare in central Tokyo |
| **Guests overnight** | House rules vary |

**House-sharing scenario:** Move-in day. You get room key, fridge shelf label, LINE group invite, and a PDF of quiet hours. Dinner someone cooks pasta. You learn names by day three.

## Costs and hidden fees explained

Planning ranges only. Tokyo central examples vary.

| Cost item | Shared house | Typical solo 1K |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly rent** | ¥55,000-¥95,000 room | ¥70,000-¥120,000+ |
| **Deposit** | 0-1 month | 1-2 months |
| **Key money** | Rare | 1 month common |
| **Agent fee** | Often none | 0.5-1 month |
| **Guarantor company** | Often none | ¥15,000-¥30,000/year |
| **Utilities** | Often included | Separate ¥8,000-¥15,000 |
| **Internet** | Often included | ¥4,000-¥6,000 |
| **Furniture** | Included | Buy or rent |

| Hidden fee watch | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| **Renewal fee** | Every 6-12 months? |
| **Cleaning fee move-out** | Fixed amount |
| **Guest fee** | Overnight visitors |
| **AC electricity** | Shared bill split? |

**Foreign residents misunderstand this:** Sticker rent vs **all-in rent**. Compare total monthly, not headline room price.

## FAQ

### Is a shared house cheaper than an apartment?

Move-in is cheaper. Monthly can be similar or higher all-in for small private apartments outside center.

### Do I need a guarantor?

Often no with operator landlord. Read contract.

### Can couples live in shared houses?

Some houses allow couple rooms. Many do not. Ask.

### Are shared houses safe?

Reputable operators yes. Check locks, cameras, reviews.

### How long can I stay?

From one month to years. Contract specific.

### Can I work from home?

Yes if room fits desk and house quiet enough.

### What if I dislike housemates?

Use common hours less, move at notice period.

### Are pets allowed?

Rare. Assume no.

### Do I need Japanese?

Helpful. English houses exist in cities.

### How do I find houses?

Operator websites, SNS groups, relocation agents.
